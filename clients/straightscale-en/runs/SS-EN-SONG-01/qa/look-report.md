## One world?
Mostly yes. Same ivory paper backdrop, same soft warm light, same charcoal and sand-cream palette, felt/knit/linen craft materials on clay across all five. Loud colour is confined to the scraps (and orange scissors, as specified). Maya (olive cardigan, bob of knit-rope, light-brown skin, slim) and the friend (teal-grey jacket, curly puff, glasses, deep brown skin, rounder) are clearly different people. Small drift: skin on both women shows fine pore-like texture that is slightly more photographic than plasticine; cloth and organizer look more like photographed fabric than clay. That is the puppet-craft lane working, not a defect, but check it against the style bible if you want it more clay.

## Overall look call
The claymation + real-craft look works for the series: characters are warm and readable at phone size (200 px thumbnails pass for every sheet), and the scraps versus calm cloth contrast carries the story.
