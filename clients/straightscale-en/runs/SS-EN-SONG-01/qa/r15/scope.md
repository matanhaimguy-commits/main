# G-SPEC round 3 scope (main session, delegated by Matan, plan 28 Oct; 29 Oct)

A FAIL is only a defect a generation would actually trip on:
- a contradiction between fields;
- impossible or overlapping geometry that changes the picture;
- a missing required region (face, hands, text surface);
- text that would render wrong;
- a breach of the look conditions (the World line word for word in every office shot, "marble" only as "no marble",
  laptop/bin/MARCH tag described in full where used, the waived-minor lines, the clay women never framed as real
  clients or results, CH-FRIEND only in her v1 standing or three-quarter view).

Anything else is an advisory: list it under suggested_spec_changes marked ADVISORY. It does not make the spec FAIL.
A safe-zone overlap that the spec states as an accepted exception with its reason is not a FAIL.
