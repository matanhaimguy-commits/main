first), the rubric and the stacks. It had no tools and saw no repo, brain, card or earlier grades. Over 19 rounds

## SHIPPED: master stack (contrarian / misdirection) · grade A
- **Visual (H-01, 0 to 3 s):** an open shop at midday in daylight, staff blurred in the back. At the owner's desk a
  hand lifts the phone in the deep red case and gives it one short shake, as if it were broken. The screen stays
  empty: no notifications, no numbers. Hands only (D1). One subject, already moving at frame 1.
- **Text (top third, 0 to about 3 s):** "Telefoni s'bie? / S'është prishur. As sezoni."
  (EN: Phone not ringing? It isn't broken. Nor is it the season.) 6 words, 2 lines.
- **Spoken (from 0.0 s):** "Telefoni nuk bie, por s'është prishur, dhe as sezoni s'e ka fajin. Po klienti yt i
  fundit, nga erdhi?" (EN: The phone doesn't ring, but it isn't broken, and the season isn't to blame either. But
  your last client, where did they come from?) Sentence 1 is 13 words.
- **Open question at the seam:** where did the last client come from? The body answers: by referral, so the
  business waits for the next one (the card's reframe, "a business that waits for the next referral"), then the send
  CTA. The answer comes after the hook, never in it.
