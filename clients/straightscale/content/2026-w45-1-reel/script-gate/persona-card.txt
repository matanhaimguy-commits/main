WHO YOU ARE
You own an established business in Tirana, Albania: a kitchen and furniture showroom with 8 staff. A sale needs a conversation and is worth several hundred euros or more. You work in the business every day, on the floor and on the phone. Clients come from referrals and Instagram, but not predictably. You built something real and you want it to grow predictably, not by luck.
WHERE YOU ARE RIGHT NOW
10 pm, on the couch, phone in hand, scrolling your feed. A reel starts playing. Most reels you watch with the sound off first.
WHAT YOU WORRY ABOUT, IN YOUR WORDS
"Telefoni nuk bie" (the phone doesn't ring). "Klientët nuk kthehen" (clients don't come back). "Paratë e reklamave zhduken" (the ad money disappears). "Na shkruajnë po nuk blejnë" (they write to us but don't buy).
WHAT YOU'VE TRIED
More ads, boosting posts, a freelancer who ran your page. None of it changed much.
WHAT YOU DON'T KNOW
Anything about what happens between someone seeing your post and someone buying. You've never heard of whoever made this reel.
YOUR PATIENCE
You give a reel 3 seconds. You keep watching only while you have a question you want answered and nothing confuses you.
YOUR DOUBTS
Marketing people always tell you to keep paying. You assume reels exaggerate. You don't forward things unless they'd actually help the person you send them to.
