## 1. Retell from the screen alone (4 sentences)

If your phone isn't ringing, it's not broken and it's not the season's fault. Think about where your last client came from. If it was a referral, your business is just sitting and waiting for the next one. The real question is what brings you clients without referrals, and a company called StraightScale wants me to send this to a friend.

## 2. After reading the captions

**What gets clearer:**
- "Pyetja e vërtetë" ("the real question") is missing on screen. With it, the 11.6 s line reads as *the point* of the reel, not just a repeated question. Without it, on screen it felt like the reel was going in circles.
- "Dhe as sezoni s'e ka fajin" ("and the season isn't to blame") is clearer than the clipped "As sezoni.", which I had to fill in myself.

**Contradictions:** none. The narration says the same thing as the screen.

**One problem:** with sound off, the small word-by-word captions *and* the big on-screen text are both running. That's two layers of text saying almost the same thing. At 0–3 s I'd read the big text and ignore the captions, but my eye jumps between them, and that costs me part of the 3 seconds.

## 3. Would I send it?

**No.** There's nothing in it that would *help* my friend. It asks the question but never says what the answer is or who can help. And sending something labelled "for a friend who still blames the season" is a small insult. I'd be telling him he's the one making excuses. I'm the one blaming the season, so the reel should be talking to me, not asking me to forward it.

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises

- **Brands/logos:** only the StraightScale wordmark on the end card. The deep red phone case shows no brand. No app screens or recognisable app UI, because the phone is always blank or dark.
- **Numbers/counts:** none.
- **Promises of speed, price or guarantee:** none. Honestly, that helps. Nothing in it made me think "here comes the exaggeration."

## 5. Most confusing moment

**The end card (15.1 s).** Up to then I was hooked. It said my exact worry, made me answer a question about my last client, and made me admit I depend on referrals. Then it asks *the* question, and instead of even a hint of the answer I get a name I've never heard and an instruction to forward it to someone else. I don't know if StraightScale runs ads, sells software, or does consulting. As someone who has already paid for ads, boosting and a freelancer, I assume the answer is "pay us to run ads," and I scroll on. One line on what they actually do differently would have kept me. Something about the part between "they write to us" and "they buy," which nobody I've paid has ever touched.

Smaller confusion: at 11.6 s the phone is "turned face up again," but I never saw it go face down, so for half a second I thought I'd missed a shot.
