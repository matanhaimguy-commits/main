### Albanian to a native ear
- **"Po klienti yt i fundit"**: "Po" as "but/and so" is natural in speech, but heard cold it could be "Yes" for half a second. It's fine in context.
- **"s'është prishur"**: natural, and a little funny, which I like.
- **"Nga një rekomandim?"**: understood, but it sounds a bit like a marketing word. Nobody here says "erdhi nga një rekomandim." We say *"e dërgoi një mik," "me njohje," "ia kishte thënë dikush."* "Rekomandimin e radhës" sounds translated.
- **"biznesi yt pret"**: "pret" can mean "waits" or "cuts." Context saves it, but it's a soft word to hear once at night.
- **"Dërgoja një pronari"**: grammatically correct, but by ear "dërgoja" is quick and the "-ja" (it, to him) can blur. With no screen I'm not sure what "it" is. "Një pronari" is also bare: owner of what? *"Një pronari biznesi"* or *"një shoku që ka biznes"* would land better.
- **Tone clash:** L1 talks to me as "yt" (you, the one with the problem). L3 suddenly treats me as the helper. Those two lines disagree about who I am.

My phone doesn't ring, and it's not because of the season or anything broken. The real cause is that my business lives on referrals, so I'm always waiting for the next one. What to do about it, the reel never says. It just asks me to send it to another owner who's waiting for the phone to ring.

"Fine, I depend on referrals, I knew that. You're the twentieth person to tell me there's a problem. Do you actually have a way to fix it that isn't 'pay more for ads,' or is this the hook before the sales call?" I also don't know who he is, so I can't even go look.

**No.** There's nothing in it that would help my friend. It only tells him his phone doesn't ring, which he knows, and sending it would basically say "hey, your business is dead," which is a little insulting.
