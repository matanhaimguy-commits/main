## 1. Retell from the screen alone
If your phone isn't ringing, the reason isn't a broken phone or a bad season. Think about where your last client came from: probably a referral. That means your business sits and waits for the next referral to happen. A company called StraightScale ("marketing that works together") made this, and wants me to send it to a friend who is waiting for the good season.

## 2. With the captions
- **Clearer:** "dhe as sezoni s'e ka fajin" (and the season isn't to blame) removes the small "the season isn't broken?" stumble at 0–3 s. "Po klienti yt i fundit" ("*but* your last client") also makes the turn smoother.
- **Contradictions:** None. The captions say the same thing as the big text.
- **Side note:** With sound off I now have two text layers, the big lines and small word-by-word captions saying nearly the same words. My eyes jump between them. It doesn't confuse me, but it's busy.
- **Still missing:** The captions also never say what StraightScale does or what the fix is.

## 3. Would I send it?
**No.** It tells my friend what their problem is but gives them nothing to do about it. If I forward it, it just says "you're waiting for luck," which is a bit of a jab and doesn't help them.

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- Brands/apps/logos: only **StraightScale's own wordmark** on the end card. The phone is in a plain deep-red case and the screen is empty, so I see no phone brand or app. (If the camera bump makes the model recognisable, someone might read it as a specific make, but nothing is labelled.)
- Numbers/counts: **none.**
- Promises of speed, price or guarantee: **none.**

## 5. Most confusing moment
**The end card (15–18.9 s).** The whole reel builds one question in my head: "then how do I stop waiting for referrals?" The end card answers with a name I've never heard and a slogan, "marketing that works together," that doesn't say with whom or how. I leave knowing my problem better but not knowing who these people are or why I'd talk to them. Also, if my last client came from Instagram rather than a referral, the 8.6 s line misses me, and nothing after it brings me back.
