### 1. Retell from the screen alone
My phone isn't ringing, and it's not because something is broken or because of the season. My last client probably came from a referral. That means my business just sits and waits for the next referral to show up. The real problem is the waiting, and I should send this to my partner the next time they blame the season.

### 2. With the captions
- **Clearer:** The logic connectors make it an argument instead of separate cards. "dhe as sezoni s'e ka fajin" (the season isn't to blame), "Atëherë" (then, if it's a referral…), "Pra" (so…). With sound, "referral → waiting" lands as cause and effect. Sound off, I had to connect it myself. I managed, but it took more effort.
- **Contradictions:** None. The captions say the same as the screen, word for word in spirit. The only mismatch is the opening shake on a blank screen, which visually suggests "broken". The caption "por s'është prishur" settles that faster than the image does.

### 3. Would I send it?
**No, but it's close.** It diagnoses my partner's excuse correctly, but it gives them nothing to do about it. Forwarding it would just be me saying "see, I told you", and that doesn't help them. If the end card said, even in one line, what the alternative to waiting is, I'd forward it.

### 4. Real brands, numbers, promises
- **Brands/logos:** Only the StraightScale wordmark on the end card, which is the reel's own brand. The phone is in a plain deep-red case. It's worth checking the real footage in case the camera layout or a logo shows through and makes the phone recognizable as a specific brand. From what's described, nothing is visible.
- **Numbers or counts:** None.
- **Promises of speed, price or guarantee:** None. That actually helps. It's the first marketing reel in a while that didn't tell me to pay more or promise "3x clients in 30 days", so my "reels exaggerate" guard stayed down.

### 5. Most confusing moment
**The cut at 15.6 s to the end card.** The whole reel builds to "it's the waiting", and I'm waiting for the answer: what do I do instead, and who are you? Instead it asks *me* to do something (send it to my partner) for a company I've never heard of and whose job it never explains. At the exact moment I was most interested, it handed me a task instead of an answer.
