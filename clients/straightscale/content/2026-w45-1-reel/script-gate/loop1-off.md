## 1. Retell from the screen alone
If your phone isn't ringing, it's not because it's broken or because of the season. Think about where your last client came from. If it was a referral, your business is just waiting for the next referral to show up. A company called StraightScale wants me to send this to another owner who's waiting for the phone to ring.

## 2. After reading the captions
**Clearer:**
- "dhe as sezoni s'e ka fajin" (the season isn't to blame) makes the short "As sezoni" obvious.
- "Atëherë biznesi yt pret…" (*then* your business waits) is the key fix. The captions spell out the if-then: *if* it was a referral, *then* you're waiting for the next one. On screen it felt like a guess followed by a statement. With "atëherë" it's logic.
- "biznesi **yt**" makes it about me personally, not business in general.

**Contradictions:** none. The captions say the same thing, just better connected. With sound off, though, I'm not sure I'd even notice small word-by-word captions under the big text. I read the big text.

## 3. Would I send it?
**No.** It tells my friend his business runs on luck and gives him nothing to do about it. Forwarding that is half an insult and zero help.

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises on screen
- **StraightScale** wordmark on the end card. That's the only brand.
- The phone is in a plain red case. No visible logo or app as described.
- **No numbers, counts, prices, speed promises or guarantees.** Good: nothing smelled like the usual "10x your sales in 30 days." That's partly why I believed it.

## 5. Most confusing moment
**The end card (12.9 s).** For 12 seconds the reel built one question: "if not the phone, not the season, then what do I do?" It gave no answer, not even a hint, and jumped to "send this to someone." I don't know what StraightScale does, and I'm asked to forward a problem I haven't seen solved, to an owner just like me. The strongest part (the diagnosis) fades because there's no next step.
