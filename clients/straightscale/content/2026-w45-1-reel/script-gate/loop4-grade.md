## Top fixes

1. **L3: replace the repeat and the ambiguous noun with a twist the viewer can't misread.** It should tie to his own want (allowed fact: clients every month, not by luck). Suggestion: "Pra, s'është sezoni. Është fati." (So it isn't the season. It's luck.) The super would read "S'është sezoni. Është fati." This removes the "reception" reading and gives a sharper payoff for the same 5 words.
2. **L2: remove the guess so it holds for referral and Instagram clients alike.** Suggestion: "Erdhi vetë? Atëherë biznesi yt pret klientin tjetër." (Came on their own? Then your business waits for the next client.) It is 8 words instead of 10, it fixes "tjetrin", and "came on their own" doesn't contradict his ads.
3. **L4: make sending an act of help, and fix the mishearing and the scope.** Suggestion: "Ia dërgo kujt të thotë 'është sezoni'." (Send it to whoever tells you "it's the season.") "Ia dërgo" can't be misheard as the imperfect, and "kujt" covers a partner, a manager or himself. The send only becomes useful once fix 1 lands. Put the same words on the end card.
4. **Length: get under 2.19 w/s break-even with headroom.** With fixes 2 and 3 plus the L1 trim in the note below, the count drops from 41 to 37 words. At 2.0 w/s: 18.5 + 0.7 + 0.6 = 19.8 s, inside the tier. At 2.3 w/s: 16.1 + 1.3 = 17.4 s.
5. **L2/L3 logic: add "therefore" before "so".** If fix 1 is not taken, the "Pra" needs a bridge (waiting → luck → not the season), or both readers will keep reading it as a trick. With "fati", the chain becomes: came on its own → you wait → it's luck, not the season.
