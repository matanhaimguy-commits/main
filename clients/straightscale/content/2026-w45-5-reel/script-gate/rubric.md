# Organic reel script gate (Script Lab, organic adapter) - the rubric

You grade ONE voiced Instagram reel script for an Albanian B2B audience (business owners). The narrator is one calm male
Albanian voice (AI voice; pace not yet measured: budget at about 2.3 words per second, and also check 2.0 words/s).
You get: this rubric, the persona card, the line table, and the reports of two separate cold readers (listen-only and
sound-off). Grade honestly: never round up, never pass a test on intent; every deduction cites a line; every pass cites
evidence. Release bar: 85/100 and no critical fail.

## Tiers (seconds of finished runtime, including the end card)
- O15: 10 to 20 s. One point (a quote reel). Rungs required: recognition, CTA (1 or 2 rungs).
- O30: 20 to 35 s. Compact shapes (myth buster ST6, short mistake list ST7, compact hidden cause ST3, demo ST14,
  comparison ST10). Rungs required: recognition, the cause in one picture line, the fix (the method), CTA.
- No full discovery story under 45 s. There is no paid rung (offer, guarantee, urgency) in organic posts: never fail a
  script for having no offer, price or guarantee.

## Gate checks (pass / fail with evidence)
1. Value per second: mark every line green (keep), yellow (trim) or red (cut).
2. Lock-in zone: the 2 to 4 sentences after the hook confirm the claim and pass a trust check (a process the viewer can
   check himself, care, relatability; there is no public proof, so no results may be claimed).
3. Loops: one master question opened in the hook and kept open until the payoff; every line leaves a question the next
   answers; "but / therefore", never "and then"; swap test (two lines that can swap = a list).
4. Payoff in the last 3 to 5 s before or with the CTA: the twist or result, and it pays the hook. Ending: a loop back to
   the hook, or a CTA that is the natural next step of the payoff. No "follow for more".
5. Facts, feelings, fun: one logic beat, one emotional beat, one twist or light moment (may be visual).
6. Sound-off read: the text hook + supers + end card alone (no voice) carry the argument; captions carry every voiced line.
7. CTA fits the card's job and cta_type (organic ladder: follow, save, send to a partner, comment KEYWORD, DM/book).
8. Ear: core sentences under 12 words, none over 17; simple words; no trips; register "ti" (informal, consistent).
9. Length: the runtime at the stated pace fits the tier (show the arithmetic at 2.3 and at 2.0 words/s).
10. Truth and hard rules (below). Any break is a critical fail.

## Hard rules (a break = critical fail)
- Facts ONLY from the allowed facts list given with the script. No counts or numbers in a line or on a screen unless in
  that list (a count of the post's own items is structure, not a claim). No invented results, clients, reviews.
- No answer-time promises or speed words (fast, within an hour, minutes, instantly, quickly). No "we answer for you",
  no "we chase your leads".
- No prices, discounts, guarantees, urgency, deadlines, hype words, em dashes.
- No real platform names or logos on screen (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Meta, etc.); generic words like "the inbox",
  "the phone", "messages" are fine. A generic sponsored label is a known risk; an unbranded mock card is preferred.
- No AI faces: people only as hands, from behind or at a distance.
- Nobody named or mocked; anecdotes only as clearly framed POV dramatizations, never as something that happened.
- For the free analysis CTA (keyword ANALIZA): only these facts may be said: free, 45 minutes, at the office in Tirana
  or at the client's business, no obligation; commenting ANALIZA gets the booking step in a DM. Never what the analysis
  covers, finds or shows.
- Never imply that boosting or ads don't work (the claim allowed: enquiries get lost after the click).

## Critical fails (any one blocks release)
1. A major confusion (not cleared within two lines) on a core rung (the cause, the fix, the CTA) in either cold read.
2. A rung the tier requires is missing.
3. A speaker or timeline break.
4. An invented fact or a hard-rule break (above).

## Scorecard (100)
| Area | Pts | How |
|---|---|---|
| Comprehension (from the cold-read reports) | 25 | Retell matches the rungs, worst reader counts (10). Confusion 10, minus 1 per minor point (cleared within 2 lines, at most -4), minus 3 per major point; 0 if major on a core rung. Scroll points: 5 none, 2 one, 0 two+ |
| Ladder and handoffs | 20 | Coverage of the tier's rungs (6). Order: each adjacent pair passes "because he now believes A, he can accept B" (6). Handoffs and master loop: 8, minus 2 per broken handoff or line with no open question |
| Hook, seam and payoff | 10 | Seam: line 2 answers or deepens the hook (4); the payoff exists and mirrors the hook (4); no bait (2) |
| Mechanism and picture | 10 | The cause/aha in one plain line (3); one picture holds through the reel (3); it explains why his usual belief or fix missed (2); explanation before claim (2) |
| Proof matched to the claim | 10 | No public proof exists: score the trust anchor (a process he can check himself, logic he can verify) right after the claim (4), the strongest real anchor used (3), the claim no bigger than the anchor (3) |
| CTA (organic) | 10 | CTA fits the job and cta_type (4); the CTA's jobs: the action, what happens next, the effort, the reason (4); match: the CTA continues the reel's promise in the same words (2) |
| Emotion and identity | 5 | Stakes he can feel (2); the lived payoff or identity (3) |
| Ear | 5 | 5, minus 1 per line over 17 words or per trip when read aloud (Albanian) |
| Length | 5 | 5 inside the tier; 2 within 10% over; 0 beyond |

## Output format (markdown, in English; quote Albanian where you cite)
1. "## Gate checks": a table, check / PASS or FAIL / evidence, for checks 1 to 10.
2. "## Line marks": a table, line / green-yellow-red / why.
3. "## Score": the table with every area's points and a one-line reason.
4. "## Critical fails": none, or the list.
5. "## Top fixes": at most 5, ranked, each with the line and a one-line fix (Albanian suggestions welcome; they will
   get a native read).
6. "## Hook stack note": does the fixed hook stack (visual, text, spoken L1) fit the body? Any part to send back?
7. The very last two lines exactly:
TOTAL: <n>/100
CRITICAL: none   (or CRITICAL: <short list>)
