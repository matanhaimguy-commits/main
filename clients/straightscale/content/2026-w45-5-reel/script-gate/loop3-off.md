**1. Retelling from the screen alone**

If your ad money disappears, look at what happens after someone clicks. The same post was shown twice: in the first, nobody is invited to write, so people look and leave. In the second, they're invited to write, you reply, you write again, and it ends in a meeting. The boost isn't to blame; what's missing is the path after the click, and StraightScale offers a free 45-minute analysis if you comment ANALIZA.

**2. After reading the captions**

What gets clearer:
- **"Ndryshon vetëm ajo pas klikimit"** (only what comes after the click changes). This is the key line, and it's missing from the screen. With it, the 3–8.5 s block would make immediate sense instead of "two identical cards, why?"
- **"i shkruan sërish"** (you write to *them* again). The "i" settles who is writing. On screen, "shkruan sërish" alone was ambiguous.
- **"i cakton takim"** (you set the meeting). This makes clear that I drive it, not that the meeting just happens.
- "Ku shkuan?" (where did they go?) is a sharper hook than the on-screen text.

Contradictions: none. The only difference is that the end card says "at our office or at your business" and the captions don't; that's extra detail, not a conflict.

**3. Would I comment ANALIZA?**

**Yes.** It's the first one that said the ads aren't the problem instead of telling me to keep paying, and all it costs me is 45 minutes, possibly at my own showroom.
(Honest hesitation: commenting a word in public feels exposed for a known business. A "or send us a message" option would make me more comfortable.)
