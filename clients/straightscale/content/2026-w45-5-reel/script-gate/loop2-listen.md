**How it sounds to a native ear:**
- *"Shiko të njëjtin postim dy herë"* is the weakest line for audio. It assumes I'm looking at the screen. With sound only, it means nothing.
- *"Klikimi s'të çon askund"* is understandable but figurative. I don't know what it means in practice.
- *"I mungonte rruga pas klikimit"* sounds a little translated, like a marketing slogan rather than how we talk. A person would say something like *"s'kishte kush t'i priste njerëzit pas klikimit"*.
- *"Pa detyrim"* is fine, but *"pa asnjë detyrim"* is more natural.
- The "ti" jumps around: in L1 it's me paying, in L3 it's the person clicking, in L4 it's me again. You don't notice it while reading, but when you hear it once, it confuses you.
- *"Paguan sponsorizimin"*, *"Ku shkuan?"*, *"cakton takim"*, *"dyzet e pesë minuta"* and *"te biznesi yt"* all sound natural.

**1. Retelling in 4 sentences**
I pay to boost posts, people click, but nobody writes or buys. According to him, the ad isn't to blame; the problem is that after the click there's no "path" that leads people somewhere. The fix is for the click to open a conversation where I reply, follow up and set a meeting. He wants me to comment "ANALIZA" so they can meet me for 45 minutes, free, at their office or mine.

**2. The doubt still in my head**
Who are these people, and what is this "path" in concrete terms? The reel never told me, so the free 45 minutes sound like a sales meeting where at the end they ask me to keep paying, just for something else. And even with the "path", someone still has to reply and follow up. The people who write to me already get replies, and they still don't buy.

**3. Would I comment ANALIZA?**
**No.** I don't know who they are. They never said a name, a result, or one real client. I'm not going to write publicly under a post that my business has a problem, for someone I can't place.

**4. The most confusing moment**
**L2 → L3, "Shiko të njëjtin postim dy herë."** The whole idea of the reel rests on the difference between "the first time" and "the second time." But it's told as something to *watch*, and I'm only *listening*. I never learned what changes in the post between the two times, so the central part of the argument passed me by.
