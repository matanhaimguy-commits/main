## 1. What the reel says (screen only)

When you pay to boost a post and the messages stay empty, the money looks like it disappeared. If you run the same post twice, the only thing that should differ is what happens after someone clicks. When the post doesn't invite people to write, they look and leave. When it invites them, and you reply, write again, and set a meeting, the boost works, so the boost isn't the problem; the missing path after the click is, and StraightScale offers a free 45-minute analysis in Tirana if you comment ANALIZA.

## 2. With the captions

**What gets clearer:**
- "Njerëzit klikojnë, dhe pastaj asnjë mesazh. Ku shkuan?" Now I know the opening means *people did click*. It wasn't that nobody saw the ad. That makes the problem sharper.
- "Shohin dhe ikin" confirms what the walking figures meant.
- "I lë takim" says *I* set the meeting. On screen it was just a calendar card.

**Anything contradicting?** Not really. One small thing: the caption "Sponsorizo të njëjtin postim dy herë" sounds like an instruction to pay for the same post twice. On screen it looked like an illustration, not advice. A sceptical owner could hear "spend double." The captions also leave out "at your business," which was the most useful line on the end card for me.

## 3. Would I comment ANALIZA?

**Yes.** It's the first marketing message I've seen that said the boost isn't to blame instead of telling me to pay for more. I'd probably look at their profile first, though, and I might send a DM rather than comment in public.

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises

- **Real company app or logo:** None. The post card is plain and unbranded. The "Mesazhet" screen is generic, though anyone will read it as Instagram/Messenger. The only brand is StraightScale's own wordmark.
- **Numbers or counts:** "1:" and "2:" as lane labels, four stops on the path, and "45 minuta." There are no results figures and no claims like "3× more messages."
- **Speed, price or guarantee:** "Falas" (free) and "pa detyrim" (no obligation). No promised speed, no promised results, no guarantee.

## 5. Most confusing moment

**3–8.9 s, the two identical cards.** The text says "only what happens after the click changes," but for about six seconds I'm looking at two identical things being clicked, and nothing changes. With the sound off, that's where I almost scrolled away. The difference only appears at 9.3 s. Runner-up is the "1:" label, which reads as "step 1" until "2:" shows up.

One more thing, as the owner: my loudest worry is "na shkruajnë po nuk blejnë." They *do* write, they just don't buy. The hook (an empty inbox) is not quite my situation. The middle of the reel ("ti i shkruan sërish") is what actually caught me. If the opening touched that sooner, I'd be hooked faster.
