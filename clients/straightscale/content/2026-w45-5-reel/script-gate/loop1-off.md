### 1. Retell from the screen alone
If your ad money disappears, look at what happens after someone clicks. The same post is shown twice. The first time, people click, look and leave. The second time, a message leads to a reply, a second message and a booking. The boost wasn't the problem; a missing piece was, and StraightScale offers a free 45-minute no-obligation analysis if you comment ANALIZA.

### 2. With captions
**What gets clearer:**
- "Paguan sponsorizimin, njerëzit klikojnë" confirms the opening tap is customers clicking *my* paid post.
- "Ku shkuan?" makes the empty inbox a question, not just a joke.
- "Ndiqe" tells me the dotted line is the customer's path.
- "Njerëzit shohin dhe ikin" confirms the left lane is people looking and leaving.

**Contradictions:** None. The captions say the same as the screen.

**What's still missing:** The captions still never name the missing piece. Hearing it doesn't add the one thing I wanted.

### 3. Would I comment ANALIZA?
**No, not tonight.** They never said what the missing piece is, so I can't tell whether it's something my team already does (we *do* reply to messages). A "free analysis" from someone I've never heard of looks like the first step of another "keep paying" pitch. Commenting publicly also tells people in my town that my ads don't work.

What would flip me: one line naming the piece, such as "someone follows up the second message within the hour," so I could recognise my own gap.

### 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- **Real platform branding:** none. There's a generic promoted card, a megaphone icon and a "Mesazhet" screen. They read as Instagram/Facebook, but there's no logo.
- **Brand:** the StraightScale wordmark, the reel's maker.
- **Numbers/counts:** "1:" and "2:" as lane labels, and "45 minuta."
- **Price:** "falas" (free), "pa detyrim" (no obligation).
- **Speed or guarantee:** none. Nothing about results, percentages or "in X days."

### 5. Most confusing moment
**12.6–17.8 s, the right lane.** In both lanes the customer clicks, but only on the right does a figure write a message. I can't tell whether the difference is the customer (who chose to write) or the business (which did something to get them writing and then replied). Then at 18 s the puzzle piece arrives unnamed, so the reel never settles it. For me that's the exact thing I came for: "Na shkruajnë po nuk blejnë." I needed to know which step is mine to fix.
