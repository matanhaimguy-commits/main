## 1. Retell from the screen only
You pay for a sponsored post, people click, and your inbox stays empty. The same post shown twice gets two outcomes: in one the click leads nowhere and people leave, in the other it opens a chat that leads to replies and a meeting. The ad itself wasn't the problem; what was missing was the path after the click. StraightScale offers a free, no-obligation 45-minute analysis in Tirana if you comment ANALIZA.

## 2. With captions
**Clearer:** "Ti përgjigjesh, shkruan sërish, cakton takim" is all "you". So *I* am the one who writes again. It's about my follow-up, not the client writing twice. That makes it much more relevant to "na shkruajnë po nuk blejnë". "Ku shkuan?" also states the question more sharply than the tumbleweed did.

**Contradiction:** It's small. On screen, the second bubble after the client writes looked like the *client's* second message. The narration says it's *me* writing again. Nothing else conflicts.

## 3. Would I comment ANALIZA?
**Yes.** It costs me 45 minutes, nothing more, and it pointed at the step where I actually lose sales: following up after they write. That isn't "boost more."

(I might send a DM instead of commenting publicly, though. I don't love announcing in my comments that my ads aren't working.)

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- **Real company apps/logos:** None. The post card is generic (drawn shopfront, megaphone), and "Mesazhet" and the calendar icon are unbranded. The only brand is StraightScale's own wordmark.
- **Numbers/counts:** "1:" and "2:" as lane labels, and "45 minuta". No stats, no "X% more clients."
- **Speed/price/guarantee:** "Falas" (free) and "pa detyrim" (no obligation). There's no speed promise, no results guarantee and no price for any later service.

## 5. Most confusing moment
**12.6–18.2 s, the right lane.** It's the payoff of the whole reel, and it packs three stops into about 5 seconds. The red line read like an error to me, and on screen I couldn't tell who sends the second message. Without sound I understood "chat → meeting" but missed the key point, which is that *I* follow up. It's also where the reel stays abstract: I saw a line light up, but not what the "path" actually is in my business.
