# 2026-w43-1-carousel · story map

**Brief.** Goal: leads. Reader: owner of an established Albanian service business, problem-aware ("I need more
followers / more ads"). Belief after: "I already lose clients between the first message and the booking, and I can
count where." Format: self-audit checklist / count list, 7 slides. Ask: comment ANALIZA (free 45-minute analysis,
funnel `analiza`, live). Based on research 2026-10-14 pick #1 (@eri.businesscoach numbered self-check, 30x median):
the mechanism (a numbered self-check about the owner), never the words.

**One-sentence argument.** Clients are not lost at the ad; they are lost after they write to you, in seven places
you can count yourself.
