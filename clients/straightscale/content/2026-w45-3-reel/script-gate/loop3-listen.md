**Albanian that sounds off to my ear**
- **L1** *"a i ftoi kush"*: understandable, but in speech I'd say *"a i ftoi njeri"* or *"a i ka ftuar kush"*. It sounds slightly translated.
- **L2** *"faleminderitin e klientit"*: turning "faleminderit" into a noun with an article sounds playful and a bit forced. More natural: *"mbarojnë me një 'faleminderit' nga klienti."*
- **L4** *"pas punës"*: heard aloud, it means "after work hours" before it means "after the job". *"pasi mbaron punimi / montimi"* would be clear.
- **L5** *"Si po të shërben kuzhina e re?"*: grammatical, but nobody in the trade talks like that. We'd say *"Si jeni me kuzhinën e re?"* or *"Si po ju duket kuzhina?"* Also, using **ti** with a client who just paid me thousands feels too familiar for many of my clients. I'd use **ju**.
- **L6** *"që u shkruan"*: for a split second it can sound like the passive "that were written". "Klientëve" fixes it, so it's minor.

## After the reel

**1. Retell in 4 sentences**
Old clients don't come back, and I assume they went to a competitor. The real reason is that after the job, the last thing in the chat is their thank-you, and nobody from my side ever writes again. The fix is to send a short message some time after the work, asking how the new kitchen is and telling them to write if they need anything. The reel asks me to forward this video to the person in my shop who handles client messages.

**2. The doubt still in my head**
A kitchen client doesn't buy again for 10–15 years, so how does "how's the kitchen?" turn into money? Maybe it's about referrals or selling them a table and wardrobe later, but he never says that. The reel promised "invite them back" and delivered "ask if they're happy". Those aren't the same thing. And I'm half afraid it just opens the door to complaints and free repair calls.

**3. Would I send it?**
**Yes, to my salesperson.** It's one concrete sentence they can copy tomorrow, and nobody is asking me for money. I'd still add my own note on top saying *what* I want from it: referrals, or the next piece of furniture. The reel doesn't say.
