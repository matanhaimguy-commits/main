**Albanian that sounds odd to my ear**
- **"Shiko ku mbaron biseda"** only works if you're looking at the screen. With audio only, "shiko" points to nothing.
- **"Kuzhina jote u montua."** You're telling the client something they watched happen. It sounds like an automatic notification, not a person. Something like "Shpresoj të jeni të kënaqur me kuzhinën e re" sounds more natural.
- **"Si po të shërben?"** is understandable, but it sounds like a translation. People say "Si po ju duket?" or "Si jeni me kuzhinën?"
- **"Jote / të / më shkruaj"** uses the informal "ti" with a client who just paid me several thousand euros. Some people talk like that in Tirana, but many showrooms would say "ju." It's a small thing that makes it sound less serious.
- **"Ia dërgo këtë video kujt u shkruan klientëve të tu"** is grammatically clunky: "ia… kujt… u… klientëve." Spoken quickly, I had to rebuild it in my head. "Atij që u shkruan klientëve" is cleaner.
- **"A i ftoi kush"** is natural and colloquial. Fine.
