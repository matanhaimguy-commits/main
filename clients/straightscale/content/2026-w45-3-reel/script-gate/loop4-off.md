## 1. Retell from the screen alone (4 sentences)
Old clients don't come back. It's not because they found someone else; it's because every chat ends at their "thank you" and nobody ever writes again. It's not your fault: after the work is done, nobody owns that conversation. So some time after you hand over the job, send one short message that mentions the work, asks how it's going, and invites them back. Then send this video to whoever talks to your clients so they start doing it.

## 2. After reading the captions
**What gets clearer:**
- "Hap bisedat e vjetra" (open the old chats) confirms that the lifted chat at 3–8 s is *me* going back. It's not the client writing. With sound off I wasn't sure until 8.6 s.
- "Pastaj, asgjë" (then, nothing) names the silence after the thank you more sharply than the blinking cursor alone.
- "Pra" (so) makes the logic clear: here's the problem, therefore send this.

**Contradictions:** None. The captions and the screen say the same thing. The only small mismatch is still the ending: the owner's hands send the message, but then "send it to the person who writes to clients." That's a gap, not a contradiction.

## 3. Would I forward it?
**Yes, to the girl at the desk who handles our WhatsApp and Instagram messages.** The reason: it gives her one exact message she can send after every delivery, starting tomorrow, and it costs nothing. I wouldn't send it to a friend with a car repair shop as easily, because "kuzhinën e re" makes it feel like it's for kitchens.

## 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- **Brand:** Only the **StraightScale** wordmark at the end. The chat app looks generic, with no WhatsApp/Instagram/Viber logo that I noticed. The red phone case is just a case.
- **Numbers/counts:** Only the tags **1 / 2 / 3**. No client counts, no percentages, no "+40% sales."
- **Promises of speed, price, guarantee:** **None.** No "results in 7 days," no price, no "guaranteed." Honestly, that's why I trusted it.

## 5. Most confusing moment
**3–8.3 s, when the old chat lifts back up.** On my phone, a chat only moves to the top when the *other person* writes. So for a few seconds I thought the reel was showing a client coming back on their own, which is the opposite of the point. It only clicked at 8.6 s, when the chat opened onto an empty reply box. If someone's thumb or a tap appeared on that chat as it lifted, I'd understand right away that *I'm* the one reopening it.
