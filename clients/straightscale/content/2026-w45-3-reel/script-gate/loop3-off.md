### 1. What the reel says, from the screen alone
Your old clients didn't come back, and you assume they went to someone else, but maybe nobody ever invited them. If you open your old chats, they all end with the client's "thank you" and nothing from you after that. The fix is to send one short message some time after the job, asking how the new kitchen is working out and telling them to write to you for anything. Send this video to whoever handles your client messages so they start doing it after the next job.

### 2. After reading the captions
**What gets clearer:**
- "A i ftoi kush **të kthehen**?" Now I know it means invite them *to come back*. On screen alone it was half a question.
- "Pas kësaj, asgjë" ("after that, nothing") says the empty reply box out loud. I understood it from the cursor, but this hits harder.
- "**Nuk e ke fajin**" ("it's not your fault"). This isn't on screen at all, and it's the line that would make me *not* feel attacked. Sound-off viewers lose it.
- "Një mesazh **të shkurtër**." Short. That makes it feel doable.

**Contradictions:** None. Captions and screen agree. One gap: the narrator never mentions the three tags (Puna / Si po shkon / Hapi tjetër). With sound on they're even more of a side thing. With sound off they're the most text-heavy moment and nothing explains them.

### 3. Would I send it?
**Yes, to my salesperson who handles WhatsApp/Instagram after delivery.** It's one concrete thing they can do tomorrow for free, and that's rare from a "marketing" reel.

(Honest doubt in my head: nobody buys a second kitchen next year. If I send it, it's because of wardrobes, other rooms and referrals, not because the reel made that case. It didn't.)

### 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- **Brand:** StraightScale wordmark at the end (the maker's own). The chat list is described as plain. As long as it doesn't look like WhatsApp (green, its layout) or Instagram DMs, no real app appears. Worth a check.
- **Phone:** red case, no visible phone brand mentioned.
- **Numbers:** only the tag labels 1 / 2 / 3. No counts, no stats, no "x% more clients".
- **Promises of speed, price, guarantee:** none. "Ca kohë" is deliberately vague. No promise that clients will come back.

### 5. Most confusing moment
**22.8–28.0 s, the three tags on the message.** It's the only moment where I have to *read* a lot at once: the full message plus three numbered labels landing on its parts, in about five seconds, at night, sound off. "1 Puna" and "2 Si po shkon" match the first sentence. But "3 Hapi tjetër" sits on "Për çdo gjë, më shkruaj këtu", which doesn't feel like a next *step*, just an open door. So right at the payoff, instead of thinking "I can do that", I'm thinking "wait, what's the third thing?" Runner-up for losing me: 3–8.3 s, where the picture doesn't change for five seconds.
