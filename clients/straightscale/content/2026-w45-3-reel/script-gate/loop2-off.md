### 1. Retell from the screen alone
My old clients don't come back, and I assume they went elsewhere, but the reel says maybe nobody invited them. It shows that every client chat just stops at their "thank you" and nothing comes after. The fix is that when the next job is finished, I send three short messages: the job is installed, how is it working for you, write to me when you need something. Then it tells me to forward this video to whoever on my team writes to clients so they start doing it.

### 2. After reading the captions
**What gets clearer:**
- "A i ftoi kush **të kthehen**?" Now "invite" clearly means invite them back. On screen, at 0–3 s, it was vague.
- "Pas kësaj, asgjë" puts words to the empty scroll.
- "**Dërgo** tri fjali" adds the verb. On screen, "tri fjali" alone could have been a headline. The captions make it an instruction.

**Contradictions:** None. The captions say the same thing as the screen, just more completely. The red thread is still never explained, not even by the narrator.

### 3. Would I send it?
**Yes, to my salesperson who handles the chats after installation.**
The one reason: it's something they can do after tomorrow's installation, it costs nothing, and the video itself is the instruction, so I don't have to explain anything.

(One private doubt: a kitchen client rarely "comes back" for a kitchen. For me this is really about referrals and extra pieces like wardrobes. The reel doesn't say that, but my salesperson will understand.)

### 4. Brands, numbers, promises
- **Brands/apps:** No recognisable app. The chat list looks generic, not clearly WhatsApp or Instagram. The red phone case shows no brand. The only brand is the **StraightScale** wordmark at the end.
- **Numbers:** "tri fjali" and the tags 1 / 2 / 3. Nothing else.
- **Promises of speed, price or guarantee:** None. It never says how many clients will come back or how fast. That's exactly why I believed it a little more.

### 5. The most confusing moment
**12.9–16.8 s, the red thread.** With the sound off it looks like a design line. I don't understand that it's meant to be "the relationship continuing". The text at the same moment repeats what I've already read, so this is the only part where I'm both confused and bored. Together with the static stretch at 3–8.3 s, that's where the reel came closest to losing me.
