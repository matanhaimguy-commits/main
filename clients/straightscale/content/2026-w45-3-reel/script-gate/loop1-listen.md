**Albanian that sounds off to my ear:**
- **L1** *"a i ftoi kush"* is fine spoken, but *"a i ftoi njeri"* sounds more natural.
- **L2** *"te faleminderit. Pas tij"*: heard without quotes, *"te faleminderit"* blurs together. *"Pas tij"* for a word feels stiff. I'd expect *"me një faleminderit. Pas kësaj, asgjë."*
- **L3** *"s'e lidhi me herën tjetër"* sounds translated. Nobody in Tirana talks like that.
- **L4** *"pas punës"* means "after work hours" before it means "after the job."
- **L5** *"Si po të shërben kuzhina e re?"* is stiff. We'd say *"Si jeni me kuzhinën e re?"* Also *ti* to a client: I'd write *ju* to someone who paid me €4,000.
- **L6** *"Dërgoja"* by ear can also sound like the imperfect "I was sending." And *atij* assumes a man.

**1. Retell (4 sentences)**
Old clients don't come back, and I assume they went to a competitor. Really, the conversation dies at "thank you" after the job, and nobody links that job to a next purchase. The fix is to send one message after the work, asking how the new kitchen is and telling them to write me when they need something. The reel tells me to send this to whoever handles my clients.

**2. Doubt still in my head**
A kitchen isn't bought twice. Will "how's the kitchen, write me if you need anything" really bring back someone who doesn't need anything for ten years? Or should it be asking for a referral? And who made this?

**3. Would I send it?**
**No.** It promised a three-part message and I only heard two. If I forward it, my employee will ask "what's the third one?" and I won't know.

**4. Most confusing moment**
**L5 against L4.** L4 makes me count to three, and L5 gives me two sentences. That's exactly where I feel I missed something, and I can't rewind. Instead of thinking about my clients, I'm thinking about whether I misheard.
