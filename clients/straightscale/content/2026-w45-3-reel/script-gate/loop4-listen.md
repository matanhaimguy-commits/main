**1. Retell (4 sentences)**
Old clients don't come back, and I assume they went to a competitor. Really, nobody ever contacts them after the job is done; the chat stops at "faleminderit" because after installation nobody is in charge of that client. The fix is to send a short message some time after delivery, asking how the kitchen is and saying I'm here when they need something. The reel asks me to forward it to whoever writes to my clients.

**2. The doubt still in my head**
A kitchen client doesn't need another kitchen soon, so "kur të duhet diçka tjetër" will mostly get a polite "faleminderit" again. What I'd actually want from old clients is referrals (a sister, a neighbor), or a reason to come back (wardrobe, table, maintenance), and the reel doesn't say that. And how long is "ca kohë"?

**3. Would I send it?**
**No.** The idea fits in one sentence, so I'd just tell my salesperson myself, and I'd rewrite the message first ("ju", "të presim", plus something concrete to come back for) instead of forwarding a text I wouldn't send as it is.
