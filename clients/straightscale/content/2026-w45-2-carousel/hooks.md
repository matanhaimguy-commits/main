## Fresh grader (G-HOOK) and rewrites
Every round used a new `claude -p` grader that saw only the AV-01 persona, the rubric and the stacks (no tools, no
repo). The first-round slide 2 was rated B-/B (17 words, abstract, a marketer's idea, not his pain). The graders
asked for his price pain ("na krahasojnë me çmimin") and for one claim shared by the cover and slide 2. Then:
- The price angle came in: "Pse të zgjodhën ty, e jo dikë më të lirë?" The headline was graded A in most rounds
  (R6, R7, R9, R10, R12). Every cover visual tried under it scored A-/B+: paper quotes with a red circle, a
  typographic strike-through, the empty message draft.
- The headline and slide 2 were swapped. The client's real moment became frame 1: in the owner's shop, the client
  looks at a cheaper "ULJE" offer on his own phone and pays anyway.

Decision rule (whole week): a channel is A when most independent fresh grades of that exact wording are A or A+
(on a tie, the latest wins) and no gate fails; my own grade must also be A.
