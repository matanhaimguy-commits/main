## D. Top 3 fixes, ranked

1. **Make B2 audible.** In L5, change "Mesazhi mbeti pa zot." to **"Copat nuk punojnë bashkë."** (The pieces don't work together.)
   - Change the super to "Nëse pritën: copat nuk punojnë bashkë" (If they waited: the pieces don't work together).
   - This keeps the line conditional (it still follows the "Nëse" sentence), removes the "pazoti" risk and echoes "punon".
   - It needs a native check.
2. **Give the end card an object and a reason.** Change L6 and the end card to **"Dërgoja shokut para se ta fikë reklamën."** (Send it to your friend before they switch off the ad.)
   - It mirrors the hook's "Para se ta fikësh", names the object for sound-off viewers and supplies the reason.
   - It is 7 words, so re-time L6 to 28.1-31.1 and hold the end card to about 32.3 s.
3. **Fix the L2→L3 handoff.** Start L3 with **"Sepse"** (Because): "Sepse reklama e sjell mesazhin. Aty i mbaron puna."
   - This makes it a therefore, ends the L2/L3 swap and turns L3's bare claim into an explanation.
   - Also smooth "Iu dha një hap tjetër?" to "I dha dikush hapin tjetër?" (Did anyone give them the next step?).

With fixes 1 and 2, the script should land in the mid 80s once a native read confirms the changes.
