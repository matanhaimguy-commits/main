**1. Retell from the picture, hook, supers and end card only**
I was about to switch off my ad, and the reel asks who answered the messages it brought. Each message needs an answer, a follow-up and a next step, but those messages sit unanswered while I'm busy with a customer in the showroom. Its point is that the ad did its job by bringing the message, and if the message waited, the problem is between the ad and the reply, so don't switch it off yet.

**2. Adding the captions**
Three things become clear:
- **There is a concrete task.** "shiko çfarë ndodhi me çdo mesazh" tells me to go and check every message. The supers never say that, and it's the most useful line in the reel.
- **"Ndjekje" makes sense.** "U ndoq kur klienti heshti?" explains that follow-up means writing again when the client goes quiet. Without the captions I didn't know what was being followed.
- **"Pjesët" stops being a puzzle.** "reklama dhe përgjigjja nuk punuan bashkë" names the two pieces: the ad and the reply. The super should just say that.

The quoted "nuk punon" also shows the reel is answering my own words, which I like.

**3. Does it say the ad works?**
Neither, and it makes it conditional. The text is "Puna e reklamës: mesazhi" and "Nëse pritën: pjesët s'punuan bashkë". It never says "the ad works" or "keep paying". The finger that doesn't press leans toward "keep it on", but it reads as "check first", not as a sales pitch, so my doubts don't kick in.

One gap: the reel assumes messages came. If my problem really is that nobody writes at all, the reel has nothing for me and I'd think "that's not me."

**4. Numbers, brands, promises**
- No number, platform name, price or logo appears, apart from the StraightScale wordmark at the end.
- No promise of speed, no guarantee.
- "Para se ta fikësh" and the red unread dots create mild pressure, but it's about timing ("check before"), not a deadline.

**5. Would I send it?**
**No, not tonight.** It points at me before it points at my friend. First I'd open my own Instagram inbox and count the messages nobody answered. If I found any, I'd more likely send it to my two salespeople than to another owner.

**6. Most confusing moment**
20.4-28.2, "Nëse pritën: pjesët s'punuan bashkë". It's the longest shot and it uses the most abstract word in the reel. "Pjesët" doesn't tell me it means my ad and my replies, and nothing happens on screen for about 8 seconds. Right after the showroom scene, which I fully understood, this is where I nearly swiped away.
