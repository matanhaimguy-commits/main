**1. Retell in 5 sentences**
I'm about to switch off my ad because I think it brings nothing. The reel says the ad may actually be bringing messages, and they just sit there while I'm busy with a client in the showroom. Its advice is to check each message before switching anything off: did someone answer it, did anyone follow up when the client went quiet, and did it move to a next step? That makes sense, because the ad only brings the message and can't answer it for me. Tomorrow I'd probably open the Instagram inbox and look at how many messages came in and how long they waited.

**2. The doubt still in my head**
I'm not sure messages even come in. My complaint is that the phone doesn't ring, and the reel just assumes the messages are there. If they are, it never told me who should answer them while I'm with a client. There's also a quieter doubt: this person probably sells some kind of "we answer your messages" service, and part two will have a price.

**3. Send / save / follow?**
I wouldn't send it. It raises the question but stops before the answer, so my friend would watch it and say "ok, so what?" I wouldn't save it either. I might follow if I knew who it was, but there was no name and nothing to tell me that part two is coming.

**4. Most confusing moment**
L5, "Por reklama dhe përgjigjja nuk punuan bashkë." It comes straight after a long "if" sentence, at normal speed. "Didn't work together" sounds smart but I can't picture it: is it a person, a system, or a time problem? Second place goes to L2's "hapin tjetër", because I don't know what the next step is meant to be.
