# Grade: StraightScale reel, "Reklama nuk punon" (O30, reach, send)

## Verdict: **FAIL**. Score 77/100, with 1 critical fail.

The words are close to shippable. What sinks it is the picture of the cause. The Line 5 shot puts the break between the ad and the phone. The spoken claim puts the break between the message and the reply. A viewer with the sound off sees "the ad isn't reaching the phone", which is the belief this reel exists to remove.

## C. Critical fails

1. **Major confusion on a core rung (cause in one picture), plus a visual continuity break.**
   - In the L5 wide shot, the gap sits between the ad's thread and the phone. That reads as "the ad isn't connecting", which is the opposite of "Your ads aren't failing."
   - It also contradicts L1, where the thread reaches the phone.

Even without this fail, 77 is below 85, so the result would still be FAIL.

## D. Top 3 fixes, ranked

1. **Move the gap (visuals for L3 and L5). This clears the critical fail.**
   - Suggested line: *"The ad's thread runs unbroken into the red phone and ends at the bubble; from each bubble a reply line starts and stops short; beside it the three circles stay empty."*
   - In L3, the thread just ends at the bubble, so the "own edge" wording goes.
2. **L3 spoken: generic, with nothing about *this* ad working.**
   - *"Sepse puna e reklamës është mesazhi. Aty i mbaron."* That is 8 words against 9, it matches the super word for word, and it gives "Sepse" a clear job.
3. **L5: keep the second sentence inside the condition.**
   - Change "Por" to "Atëherë": *"Atëherë reklama dhe përgjigjja nuk punuan bashkë."* Word count and timing stay the same.

**Smaller items:**
- Measure the narrator's pace before you voice.
- Correct the word count to 65.
- Get a native read on "Mori hapin tjetër?" and on "klienti" being used for two people.
- Consider "Dërgoja shokut para se ta fikë reklamën." on the end card, so it says the same thing as the spoken CTA.
