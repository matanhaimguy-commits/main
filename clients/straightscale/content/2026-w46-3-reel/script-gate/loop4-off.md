**1. Retelling (no captions)**
I'm about to switch off my ad, but the reel says the ad did bring messages and asks who answered them. It shows that the ad's job ends at the message, and that while I'm busy with a customer in the showroom, those messages sit unanswered and go cold. If they waited, the problem wasn't the ad but what happened after it, so don't switch it off yet, and send this to a friend.

**2. Adding the captions**
Several things get clearer:
- "Reklama 'nuk punon'?" quotes my own belief, so it hooks me harder.
- "U ndoq kur klienti heshti?" (followed up when the customer went quiet?) explains the bare "Ndjekje?".
- "Aty i mbaron" (it ends there) explains the marker.
- "Ti je me një klient tjetër" confirms the hands are mine.
- "Reklama bëri punën e saj" (the ad did its job) is a stronger statement than any super.

Nothing contradicts what's on screen.

**3. Works, doesn't work, or neither?**
Neither, with a condition. The test is "Nëse pritën: problemi ishte pas reklamës" (if they waited, the problem was after the ad). But the hook "Reklama solli mesazhe" (the ad brought messages) states as a fact that the ad delivered. For someone sure that nothing came, that's an assumption the reel never earns. Only the captions say outright "reklama bëri punën e saj", and only after "if".
