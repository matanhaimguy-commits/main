**1. Retell in 5 sentences**
I think my ad doesn't work because the phone doesn't ring and the money disappears. The reel says the problem may not be the ad at all: maybe people wrote, and the messages sat unanswered or with no follow-up while I was busy with a customer. Its method is to go back through every message the ad brought and check three things: was it answered, was it followed up when the person went quiet, and was there a next step. That makes sense, because the ad can only bring the message, and if I'm on the floor with someone else, nobody is handling it. Tomorrow I'd open the Instagram inbox and count how many messages came last month and how many we actually answered and chased.

**2. The doubt still in my head**
What if the messages never came at all? Then the ad really doesn't work, and this was a nicer way of saying "keep paying." Even if they're right, they told me where the problem is but not how to fix it when I have no free person to sit on messages. And I don't know who's talking or what they want to sell me afterwards.

**3. Send / save / follow?**
**No, I wouldn't send it.** It gives a good question but no answer, and I don't forward half-things to other business owners. I'd check my own messages first, and if it turned out to be true I'd tell my friend myself. **Save: no.** **Follow: no.** I don't even know whose account it is, and nothing in what I heard gave me a reason.

**4. Most confusing moment**
**L3: "Sepse puna e reklamës është mesazhi."** Heard once, "mesazhi" first sounds like the ad's *message*, meaning its content, which is what marketers always talk about. It takes a beat to understand they mean the DM a customer sends. The "sepse" also doesn't clearly answer the three questions before it. Close second: L5, which is long and conditional and hard to hold after one listen.

**5. Does my ad work, not work, or neither?**
**Neither.** It's conditional: *"Nëse mesazhet erdhën dhe pritën, reklama bëri punën e saj."* It only works *if* messages came. It never says what to conclude if they didn't.

**6. Selling / speed / price / guarantee / hurry?**
No price, no speed, no guarantee. But the "don't switch it off" part has that marketing-person smell: *"Para se ta fikësh…"* (L1) and again *"para se ta fikë reklamën"* (L6). That's exactly what everyone who wants me to keep paying says. It's a mild push to wait, not an aggressive one.

**7. Blame on me or my staff?**
A little, but softened. L2 (*"Mori përgjigje? U ndoq kur klienti heshti?"*) makes me feel checked up on, because the honest answer is no. L5 (*"problemi ishte pas reklamës"*) means the problem is us. L4 (*"ti je me një klient tjetër"*) saves it: it says I'm busy, not lazy. So it reads more like "you're too busy" than "you're bad at your job."
