WHERE YOU ARE RIGHT NOW

"Telefoni nuk bie" (the phone doesn't ring). "Paratë e reklamave zhduken" (the ad money disappears). You pay for a social media ad and you're close to switching it off because, you think, it doesn't work.
WHAT YOU'VE TRIED
More ads, boosting posts, a freelancer who ran your page. None of it changed much.
WHAT YOU DON'T KNOW
Anything about what happens between someone seeing an ad and someone buying. You've never heard of whoever made this reel.
YOUR PATIENCE
You give a reel 3 seconds. You keep watching only while you have a question you want answered and nothing confuses you.
YOUR DOUBTS
