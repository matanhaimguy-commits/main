### 1. The reel in 5 sentences
- **Problem:** I think my ad doesn't work, and I'm about to switch it off.
- **Real cause:** The ad may have brought messages, but nobody answered them in time, nobody followed up when the client went quiet, and nobody gave them a next step, so they were left "pa zot."
- **Method:** Before I switch anything off, I should go through every message the ad brought and check what happened to each one: answered, followed up, given a next step.
- **Why it makes sense:** The ad's job ends when the message arrives, and I'm busy on the floor all day, so messages sit and wait.
- **What I'd do now:** Tomorrow morning, before I touch the ad settings, I'd open the Instagram inbox for the last month and count how many messages came in and how many we really dealt with.

### 2. The doubt still in my head
My problem is "telefoni nuk bie," and this reel talks about messages. What if almost nobody messaged? Then the ad really doesn't work, and this was a nice way to tell me to keep paying. Also, the messages I do get are mostly "sa kushton?" from people who disappear. Are those even real clients? And I'm waiting for the catch. Someone made this to sell me something, maybe a chatbot or a receptionist service, and they didn't say what.

### 3. Would I send it?
**No, not to a "partner."** I don't have one. I'd maybe show it to the girl who handles our Instagram, as in "look, check this." I'd save it so I remember to check the inbox. I wouldn't follow, because I never heard who made it.

### 4. Most confusing moment
**L5.** I'd been waiting for the "so what do I do," and instead I got the same idea a third time, plus an "if" ("nëse mesazhet erdhën") about something I don't know. "Pa zot" is a strong phrase, but it raises the question it doesn't answer: whose job should it be? A smaller confusion was L2's "kur heshti," where I had to work out who went quiet while the next question was already playing.

### 5. Does my ad work, not work, or neither?
**Neither, but it leans toward "your ad probably works."** "Nëse mesazhet erdhën dhe pritën, reklama bëri punën e saj." It's conditional. It never says the ad works. It says if messages came, the ad isn't the problem.

### 6. Did anything feel like selling or pushing?
There's no price, no speed promise and no guarantee. The push I felt was "Para se ta fikësh…" (L1) and "para se ta fikni reklamën" (L6). That's "don't switch off the ad," which is exactly what marketing people always tell me. It's mild, but my guard went up.

### 7. Did anything blame me or my staff?
Not directly. L4 actually lets me off the hook: "Por ti je në punë, dhe mesazhi pret." That's fair. But L2, "U përgjigj dikush? E ndoqi dikush?", made me feel caught, because the answer is probably "no, nobody." And "Mesazhi mbeti pa zot" means someone in my shop dropped it, even if the reel doesn't say who.
