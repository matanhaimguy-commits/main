## Custom principles (evidence ids are the lines above)
1. **Name the channel the owner already relies on, in the first 2 words** ("Cold email?" 12.5x, "Cold email." 4.4x).
   Spoken and text.
2. **A verdict beats a tip.** A ranking or tier list, a judgment on each fix, wins (3 of the top 7 topic winners); a
   generic how-to or "3 reasons" tip loses (Kallaway 0.30x, 0.46x). Visual: one bold graphic object that judges, over
   or beside the person.
3. **Time or money already lost, counted** ("six months of making content that doesn't get you any clients" 3.5x;
   "50k a month... still poor" 3.6x). Numbers only from the buyer's own words or the brand.
4. **An accusation without a picture loses** ("You don't make content." 0.18x). Aim at the mistake, never the viewer.

## Format library (song entries for this page)
- **Named-person story** (song swipe 06, levelfifty) · "[Name]'s [moment in their words], after [the contrast]" ·
  text: the moment in 4 to 8 words · visual: [the owner] + [the month on the wall calendar] + [the problem moving] +
  [home-office persona cue]
- **Characters explain** (song swipe 09) · "I'm the [failed fix] she [did], and I'm [the failure in the picture's
  words]" · text: what she keeps doing and that it doesn't hold · visual: [the fix as a talking patch] + [on the quilt
  of her year] + [coming loose] + [the owner behind, at her desk]

## Do not use
Generic tips ("use more CTAs", "3 reasons your content isn't driving leads"); "you don't [x]" accusations; banned hook
words from the strategy (Patchwork, agency, done-for-you, funnel, qualified, guaranteed, growth partner, AI); real
platform logos or names on screen.
