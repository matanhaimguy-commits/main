# Cold read 1 · SS-2026-11-W1-04 (fresh claude -p reader, persona AV-01, no tools, saw only the persona and the copy; 28 Oct 2026)

Reader 1 prompt: persona card + the slides in order with what each picture shows (the six position questions)

**A. Retell (5 sentences)**
The post says people compare you on price when they see nothing else about you, because all the work photos look the same. It then gives five questions to check before you lower your price. Does your first line say who you work for? Do you say something your competitor doesn't? Are the words on your page your customers' or yours? Is the step after someone writes visible, and do you give a reason besides price? Every "no" is a missing line, and if I comment ANALIZA I get a free 45-minute analysis, in Tirana or at my business, with no obligation.

**B. After the cover**
On the cover I wanted to know what to check before I lower my price, because that is exactly what I'm tempted to do. Slide 2 only partly pays it off. Yes, it names the cause: "Të krahasojnë me çmimin kur s'shohin gjë tjetër". That is new to me, because I always thought my price was simply too high. But it's a statement, not yet a check, so it's a small payoff and the real one starts at slide 3.

**C. Where I stop swiping**
I read to the end. The questions are short and I recognise myself in them, especially slide 6 and "mesazhet mbeten pa përgjigje".

**D. The turn**
Slide 2. It moves the blame from "my price is too high" to "they see nothing else". Everything after it follows from that.

**E. What I believe now, and what I'd do**
I believe, maybe 60%, that my price isn't the whole problem and that my page looks like everyone else's. I would open my Instagram profile and check the first line and whether there's a next step. I'd probably still think I need more ads. I would not lower the price tonight.

**F. Most confusing slide**
Slide 4 (the two phones). "Çfarë bën ndryshe" is hard for me to answer. I do the same job as the others, so what is different? The picture doesn't help. Slide 7 is a bit abstract too: what is "një arsye pa çmim" for a windows or kitchen business? Is it warranty, delivery time?

**G. What I do**
I would save it and look at it again tomorrow at the shop. I might comment ANALIZA if I was sure it's really free. I wouldn't send it to anyone, because I don't want others to see I have this problem. I'd check the page first, and the profile and the comments, to see if they are real people.

**H. Odd or untrustworthy lines**
"Analizë falas, 45 minuta, pa detyrim." Free always makes me think there will be a sales pitch at the end, though the "pa detyrim" helps. Nothing else sounds hyped, and there are no numbers I can't trust.
