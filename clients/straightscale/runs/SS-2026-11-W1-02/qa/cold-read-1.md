Reader 1 prompt: persona card + the slides in order with what each picture shows (the six position questions)

**A. Retell**
Customers compared the price and still came to you, and the carousel asks why. The answer is not in your head. It sits with the people who just bought. It asks three yes/no questions: do you know why they chose you, do you have the answer in their words, and have you written it down. One "no" is enough to continue. So you send the last buyers one question, "what made you ready to choose someone else?", and then put their exact words into your next post and your reply message.

**B. Slide 1 and slide 2**
Partly yes. Slide 1 made me curious because it is my situation: people compare prices and still buy from me, and I don't really know why. Slide 2 gives me "Pse ty, e jo dikë më të lirë? Pyeti ata." That is a real answer: ask them. But it is only a short answer, and the real payoff comes later on slides 6 and 7. So slide 2 is a good hook but not a full payoff.

**C. Where I stop swiping**
Slide 3. "E di pse të zgjodhën klientët e fundit? Po apo jo?" I honestly don't know. I think it's the price, or the quality, but I have never asked. That hit me. I kept reading to the end, though, because slide 6 gave me something I could do tonight.

**D. The turn**
Slide 3. Up to then it looked like a story about price. Here it becomes a question about me: I don't actually know why they pick me. Slide 6 is the second turn, where the answer becomes an action.

**E. What I believe now, and what I'd do**
I believe my problem may not be that my price is too high or that I need more followers. I may simply not know what my buyers value, so my posts and replies say generic things. Tonight or tomorrow I'd message 5 recent clients: "Çfarë të bëri gati të zgjidhje dikë tjetër?" Then I'd write down their answers in a notebook. I would not expect it to fix the quiet phone by itself, though.

**F. Most confusing**
Slide 6. The wording "Çfarë të bëri gati të zgjidhje dikë tjetër?" is hard to follow: it literally asks what made them ready to choose someone else, when I want to know why they chose me. It took me a second read to see that it means "what almost made you go elsewhere?" Slide 5 was also a bit odd: "Një jo mjafton për të vazhduar" is clear, but the image of a closed notebook doesn't add much.

**G. Action**
I would save it. I'm busy at work and I don't comment on posts, but I would want to come back to slide 6 when I sit down to write the next ad. I'd probably not follow the page yet, because it has not shown me proof that it works. If it is still good in a few posts, I'd follow it.

None that promise a result or give numbers. The only thing that sounds off is the confusing phrasing on slide 6 mentioned above. Also, "Kopjoji ashtu siç i thanë" (copy them exactly as they said it) is good advice, but I'd worry a bit about quoting clients without asking them first.
