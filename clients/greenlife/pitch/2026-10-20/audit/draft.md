## Skeptical owner read: top 3 and the fixes (applied in the decks)

1. **today, "2 phone numbers, 3 addresses"**: he saw one number on the site (written two ways) and one address.
   Fix: only leaks he can open on his phone, with where each one shows: "067 on the site, 069 on Google and
   Facebook". The addresses move to the notes. "0 €" becomes "0 ways to book a test on your site" on the slide; the
   "0 €" product prices go in the notes as "no prices shown".
2. **cost, "1 home in 100 = €1.4M"**: a made-up share and a cheaper competitor's price, 3.5x his whole yearly
   volume. Fix: start from his own count (10,000 installs in 15 years, or 7,000 in 10, both ≈ 700 a year), then
   show what +20 systems a month in Tirana and +10 elsewhere are worth at an example €1,000 ticket, and what one
   booked test may cost for that to pay. His real price and margin replace the example live.
3. **angles and offer, "measured before and after"**: he doesn't test after install today, and his filter keeps
   minerals, so a TDS meter barely moves. Fix: "Ujë i Matur" is shown as a proposal with a 20-home pilot before any
   ad runs (footer on the angles slide). The test names what moves: free chlorine and smell at the tap, hardness
   where a softener is fitted, and an optional lab test for bacteria. Never TDS. Who runs it and the cost per test
   go under "Confirm in the meeting".
   Also: "up to 40 years" everywhere (never "40 years" flat, never "4x"), and what it covers is confirmed first.

- **Dominant desire** (top theme, L score 36): certainty that the water the family drinks is safe, without depending on
  anyone. L34 "Nuk jam e sigurt për cilësinë", L36 "Për fëmijët duhet të blej ujë të ambalazhuar", L8 "Per te qene me
  te sigurt". Broad layer agrees: B4 "makes the water actually safe for my kids to drink".
- **Identity frame:** the responsible parent who stopped waiting and decided for his home. L36, L38 ("për të
  mbijetuar dhe jo jetuar"), L6 "Flitet per shendet, jo per para". (inference on the "stopped waiting" part, built
  from L39 to L42: confirm in the meeting.)
- **Ranked fears:** 1 invisible contamination reaching the kids (L34, L36, L62) · 2 abandoned or lied to after the
  sale (L1, L42, B44) · 3 paying twice or overpaying, pressure and deposits (L37, L2, B8) · 4 the filter doesn't
  really work (L3 ISHP test "si ai i pa filtruari") · 5 water ruining the boiler and washing machine (L58).
- **What the fear costs today:** "2400 lekë në muaj" for bottles, "Rreth 5000 lekë" with the bill (L37); "100 mijë
  lekë në vit" for a filter (L34); "rezistencën e bolierit e kam ndërruar dy herë" (L58); "3 orare" waiting (L1).
- **Market stage:** solution aware, sophistication 5 (6 clichés said by all, Green Life included). Lead with identity
  and a named mechanism.
- **Gaps (5 checked):** proof after install, re-done at every service: 0 of 5 (Camelot, outside the 5, claims it) ·
  no electricity, no wasted water, stainless: 0 of 5 · published customer test numbers and 10-year owners: 0 of 5 ·
  developers and rental hosts: 0 of 5 with an offer (Albuji names new buildings in one line) · risk reversal tied to
  proof: 0 of 5. 0% financing: only Green Life of the 6.
- **Unfair advantage:** warranty up to 40 years vs next best 10 (ALBAFILTERS) and 1 (Aquafilter).
- **Named standard:** **"Ujë i Matur" (Measured Water)**: tested at your tap before, after install and at every
  service, numbers in writing. A proposal: Green Life talks about "parametrat e ujit" (FB 6 and 15 Oct) but does not
  test after install today. Confirm before any ad runs.
