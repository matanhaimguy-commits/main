**Verdict: yes, installers are the likely bottleneck once tests are booked daily.** The market leader trains people
with no experience, which means experienced installers are scarce. Green Life's own team size is unknown: ask how
many installs a week it can take today. Plan: a recruiting lead-form ad in the same ad account, budget `[€__]`
(paid and plan notes only).

## Market stage

- **Awareness:** solution aware. Buyers name the pain (turbid, chlorine smell, bidona, paying twice) and the solution
  type (filters, depozita, RO); few name brands except in reviews. Low awareness segments (new parents, new movers)
  start from the trigger moment.
- **Sophistication: 5.** All 9 companies say "ujë i pastër/i shëndetshëm"; 5 of them use "99%"; 5 say "certified";
  5 sell a country of origin; 6 say "stop buying bottles". Green Life repeats all six clichés.
- **Rule applied:** lead with identity (the parent who stopped waiting and decided for his home) and a named
  mechanism ("Ujë i Matur": measured at your tap before, after install and at every service). Never "cleaner, better".
