# Green Life Albania: competitor gap matrix (v3)

Research date: 20 Oct 2026. Sources: each company's own site (read with Exa web_fetch), Google Maps (Apify compass/crawler-google-places, 7 places, up to 100 newest reviews each), Meta Ad Library (Facebook Ads MCP `ads_library_search` plus Apify `apify/facebook-ads-scraper` to read ad text), Green Life's Facebook posts through the healthandbeauty1.com mirror, and job boards.

**The 5 competitors used, and why:** Blue Filters AL (most Google reviews), Albuji (most recent reviews and the only one of the 5 with ad copy we could read), Water Boutique (biggest showroom, has its own lab), ALBAFILTERS (only one with a price list and a subscription), Aquafilter (closest match to Green Life's "Made in USA" under-sink pitch, and shows prices).
- **Blue FILTERS Tirana** (bluefilters.com, 4.8, 60 reviews) is Blue Filters Albania's second office. blue-filters.al lists "Rruga Shkelqim Fusha ... bluefilterstirana@gmail.com", which matches the Maps listing. It has its own review row below but is not counted as a separate competitor. Its listed website, bluefilters.com, is the global brand site, and the copy we fetched contained injected casino spam.
- **Blue Filters AL and Water Boutique are run by the same people.** Xheni Vata is administrator of both (LinkedIn; businessmag.al, 9 Oct 2025). They sit on the same street, Rruga Vangjel Noti. TUZ ECO LIFE is their wastewater department and uses the same phone number, +355 69 205 0564. So the 5 "competitors" are really 4 independent companies.
- The 3 Meta advertisers (Clean water, Filtra Uji me Qera1, Mist Water Filter) are in a separate table, since only their ads could be read.

## 2. Gaps (from the matrix only)

Base: **Green Life plus the 5 competitors**, which are really 4 independent companies because Blue Filters AL and Water Boutique share management. Where noted, the 3 Meta advertisers are added (8 non-client companies in total).

**Unclaimed promise: "We prove it on your own tap, before and after."** Pre-sale testing is common: 3 of 5 offer it explicitly (Water Boutique free, Albuji, ALBAFILTERS), and Blue Filters has a lab. **A water test after install that shows the result, re-done at every service visit, is claimed by 0 of 5 competitors and 0 of 3 advertisers, and Green Life doesn't claim it either.**
- Caveat: Camelot, outside the set, does claim before-and-after testing. So this is open among the companies that hold Tirana's Google reviews and ad space, but not across the whole market.
- Supporting evidence: an Albuji 1-star review shows a customer paying for an ISHP lab test after install and finding bacteria.
- Green Life's own posts already raise the question ("Çfarë parametrash ka realisht në uji në shtëpinë time?") but don't answer it with a test.

**Unclaimed mechanism: a filter that wastes no water and needs no electricity, in a stainless steel body.** 0 of 5 competitors and 0 of 3 advertisers say this. Their mechanisms are RO membranes (Blue Filters, Albuji, ALBAFILTERS, Aquafilter, SafeWater), UV lamps (Water Boutique, ALBAFILTERS), vertical flow (Blue Filters) and softeners.
- Green Life already says "100% pa energji elektrike", "duke mos shpërdoruar asnjë pikë" and "Çelik inox 18/10", but only on one service page. It isn't in the headline or the ads.
- The NSF-certified 0.2-submicron silver ceramic stage is also unique to Green Life. Blue Filters lists NSF for its brand, but not for a specific ceramic stage.

**Unclaimed proof: published test numbers from real customers' taps, and systems still working after 10 or more years.**
- 0 of 5 publish any customer's before/after water numbers. They rely on certificate lists, years in business and client counts.
- 0 of 5 show long-term customers on camera. Green Life's FB already has "Më shumë se 10 vite përdorim" (8 Oct 2026) and "Mbi 5 vite përdorim" (13 Oct 2026), and a Google review says "I have been using it for the last 7 years". None of this is on the website.
- Green Life's "deri në 40 vite" warranty is by far the longest stated: the next is ALBAFILTERS at 10 years, then Aquafilter at 1 year, and the other 2 give no length. But Green Life doesn't publish the terms.

**Unserved audience: residential developers / builders, and short-term-rental hosts.**
- B2B is well served overall. Hotels and restaurants are named by Water Boutique, Albuji and ALBAFILTERS, and Blue Filters lists businesses. Offices are named by Aquafilter (Office Star) and Albuji. Bars and cafés are named by ALBAFILTERS and Water Boutique.
- Developers: only 1 of 5 (Albuji) mentions "ndërtesat rezidenciale të reja", and only as one line in a list of sectors served. 0 of 5 have a developer offer, such as whole-building filtration as a selling point for new apartments.
- Short-term-rental / Airbnb hosts: 0 of 5 mention them.
- This fits Green Life well: its WH500/WH750 whole-house systems are "Ideale për përdorim familjar dhe profesional".
- Limit: we read the homepage plus 1–2 pages per competitor, not every sector page.

**Offer gap: risk reversal tied to proof.**
- 0 of 5 and 0 of 3 advertisers offer a money-back promise, a trial period, or "if the test doesn't improve, we remove it".
- Green Life is the only one of the 6 with **0% financing**. That offer lives only in FB posts that expire on 30 Oct 2026. The website shows "0 €" on every product and says nothing about financing.
- Where Green Life trails:
  - **Visible prices:** 2 of 5 (ALBAFILTERS, Aquafilter) and all 3 advertisers show them.
  - **Subscription or rental:** ALBAFILTERS (120 €/yr) and SafeWater (12,900 lekë/yr, "35 Lekë/Ditë") offer one. Green Life has neither.
- Gap to fill: a standing, on-site offer that combines the monthly price, the free before/after test and service years, as a permanent offer rather than a 2-week promo.
