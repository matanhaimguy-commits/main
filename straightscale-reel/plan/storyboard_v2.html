StraightScale · Patchwork Marketing · storyboard v2

Idea: your marketing looks like a ransom note glued together from scraps (everyone writes "cilësi"). StraightScale is one straight gold line that cuts through it. After the cut, everything sits on that line: the lesson, the promise, the logo, the offer. Frames below are real renders from the rough cut (voice only, music and sound effects come after approval).

WORLD 1 · THE PROBLEM (shots 1–5)Torn paper, tape, label-maker strips, ransom letters cut from 6 styles of our two brand fonts, marker strikes, rubber stamps, camera shakes and zooms. Loud, crooked, crowded.
WORLD 2 · THE STRAIGHT LINE (shots 6–13)Marble, one gold line at the same height in every shot. Words rise out of the line or drop from it. Straight edges, aligned, calm. Playfair for big words, Montserrat for everything else.

Problemi nuk është çmimi.

The problem isn't the price.

Hard cut to crimson paper. Giant ÇMIMI, wider than the frame, struck out with black marker.

034.7–9.55 s

Problemi është që flet njësoj si të gjithë: "cilësi", "çmimet më të mira".

The problem is you sound like everyone: "quality", "best prices".

PATCHWORK: torn scraps taped together (site by a freelancer, ads by an agency, Instagram by the nephew, phone by whoever's nearby). Every scrap spells CILËSI. A stamp slams: ÇMIMET MË TË MIRA.

Rreth një mijë e tetëqind reklama aktive në Shqipëri thonë "cilësi".

About 1,800 active ads in Albania say "quality".

Pull back: the collage is one of 1,800 identical boards. Counter rolls 1.000 → 1.800, source under it (Meta Ad Library, 11.10.2026). On "cilësi" a red sticker ripples over every board.

0513.6–17.4 s

Kur të gjithë thonë të njëjtën gjë, klienti krahason vetëm çmimin.

When everyone says the same thing, the customer only compares price.

Dive back in. All scraps re-slap the exact same word. Black marker redacts everything the customer stops reading. Only red € stickers survive (no amounts).

0617.4–18.5 s

Ja çfarë bën.

Here's what you do.

One straight gold line slices the collage. Both halves fall away to clean marble. The line settles level and stays to the end: that's StraightScale.

0718.5–22.65 s

Lexo komentet dhe vlerësimet e klientëve, për ty dhe për konkurrentët.

Read customers' comments and reviews, about you and your competitors.

"Lexo" rises out of the line. Review tapes scroll under it; the key phrase in each gets highlighted as it passes. Tabs: TI / KONKURRENTËT. Reviews are abstract (stars + bars), no invented quotes.

0822.65–26.6 s

Gjej çfarë duan vërtet dhe ktheje në një premtim që s'e bën askush.

Find what they really want and turn it into a promise nobody makes.

The highlights fly into one point and compress into a gold square (the logo's square). "një premtim" drops out of the line. Pull back: everyone else's lines are crooked and grey.
