## 3. Pre-write pack (collapsed)

**Structure pick.** Stamp: problem aware / sophistication 4. Load-bearing belief: rung 4 "she books the first good answer"
(she can't see the real cause). Story tier ST5 Two futures (three owners, one difference) on a narrator, because the
difference between the owners IS the cause, and it can be shown, not claimed. Short tier ST6 Myth buster (posting more,
"we'll answer soon"), because at C45 there is no room for a story (router rule 2). Spines: A = ST5 (picked), B = ST6
(C45), C = ST2 PAS on an owner confession (dropped: needs a real client's story, the proof bank is empty).
Rungs cut on purpose: 8 client proof (none documented; replaced by a live demonstration the viewer runs), 11 guarantee
(the offer is free; "no obligation" is in the caption), 12 urgency (no real deadline exists; none invented).

**Because sentences (S60).** Because she sees one woman asking three businesses at once (1), she can feel the owners who
are asleep lose her (2). Because she watched the sleeping third owner win anyway (2, 7 shown), she accepts that the first
good answer wins, not the best business (4). Because the cause is speed, not quality, she can accept it's not her fault
she can't answer in a minute (3), and that posting more and "we'll answer soon" don't touch that cause (3). Because her
fixes miss the cause, she wants what the third owner has (5), which the script then names (7) and explains part by part
(6). Because each part answers a fear (robot: his words; control: the alert), she can accept the brand (7). Because she
doubts the brand, the proof is a test she runs herself (8), and only then the offer (10).

**Bible.** Speakers: one narrator (Voice 1, the StraightScale strategist, "we"), seen on camera from L14; "she" = the
customer in L1 to L10, "a client" from L11 (the switch is marked by the noun), "you" = the viewer. Timeline: one night
(22:47 on screen) → before midnight → the morning. Picture: the race between three phones; the assistant obeys it (it
makes the owner first). Word list: ANALIZA (the keyword, said as "comment ANALIZA", its meaning shown on screen:
"Analizë falas"); StraightScale (named at L13 as "we"). Setups and payoffs: three businesses (L1) → (L16); asleep (L2/L3)
→ "to be first at night" (L8); "we'll answer soon" (L7) ← persona's own auto-reply. Facts: brief.md F1 to F9, every one
sourced; no line restates a fact in new numbers.

**Reveal plan.** Carrier: the third owner (reason: it's how he won her). Shown working: L3 (phone answers while he
sleeps). Bridge: L8 to L9 (to be first at night you need what he has). Each part's job: L10, L11, L12. How to use: n/a
(done for you; the analysis is the next step). Named: L13, after it was shown and explained.
