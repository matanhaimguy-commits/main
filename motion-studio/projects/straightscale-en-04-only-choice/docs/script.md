## Final draft (loop 4): 165 words, about 66 s at 2.5 words/s (O60: 45 to 75 s)
Text hook (approved, 0 to 3 s, top third): A lower price won't stop "cheaper elsewhere"
```
Your lower price isn't enough if they can get it cheaper.
A lead asked you one thing: why you, and not the others?
So you dropped your price twice and threw in a bonus.
They still wrote: "We can get it done cheaper from someone else."
You'd lost them before you dropped a cent.
Look at how you describe what you sell.
Yours sounds like everyone else's: a program, weekly calls, a promise.
So all they could compare was price.
That week, another coach got asked the same thing.
She didn't touch her price.
Her answer started with the exact sentence her clients use for their problem.
Then she showed how she works, in steps she named herself.
Nobody else has those steps, so they can't just compare price.
You got "cheaper from someone else."
Her lead wrote back: "Can you walk me through those steps?"
Not signed yet. But price never came up.
Follow for the next part: where leads like hers come from.
```
