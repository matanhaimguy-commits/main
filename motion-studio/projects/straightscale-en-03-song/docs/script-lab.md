## 2. Persona card (the cold readers' only input besides the script)
You run a founder-led expert business: a consultant, coach or small agency owner, late 30s to mid 40s, English-speaking,
about $20K to $50K a month, working from home. Your best clients came by referral and you like them. It's late evening;
you're on the couch, phone in hand, scrolling Instagram Reels about business growth, sound off at first. A reel starts
playing in your feed. What you believe, in your words: "some months we'd sign 3 clients, other months literally
nothing"; "LinkedIn works for me, but it's sporadic and not predictable"; you think you need a better channel. What
you've tried: cold email at higher volume, LinkedIn, a coach, some ads; each helped for a while. What you don't know:
how the separate parts of getting clients fit together. Your doubts: you're tired of "post more", "more outreach",
"fix your offer", and you distrust anything that sounds like an agency pitch or "done-for-you". You give a reel 2 to 3
seconds and keep watching only while you have a question you want answered and nothing confuses you. Would you send it,
save it or follow?

## 4. Belief ladder (O60) and the because test
| Rung | Belief in her head | Installed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | "That's my year: a full month, then an empty one." | Maya's full March and silent April (hook stack, scene 1) |
| 2 Stakes | "It costs me: when it's full I'm too busy to look for the next one, and when it's empty I wait on referrals." | scene 2, the accommodation, her words (Q-R-0063, Q-R-0065) |
| 3 Release | "What I tried each worked for a month, then came loose. It isn't me." | the fixes, each killed with its reason |
| 4 Aha | "So the cause is that every fix was sewn on alone, joined to nothing." | the spoken turn, then the chorus: THE PICTURE, named Patchwork Marketing |
| 5 Hope | "Steady months don't need one more patch." | one line, the angle |
| 6 Mechanism | "Pieces sewn to each other, in order, hold: her buyers' words, an offer nobody can compare, posts that bring those buyers, a page, then ads only on what worked." | verse 2 |
| 8 Payoff (process) | "When the next patch is sewn to the last one, a quiet month doesn't empty the year." | scene 1 replayed as a process: the April patch sewn onto March's, and it holds |
| CTA | "Someone I know needs this." | send |

Because sentences:
- 1 to 2: Because she sees her own full-then-empty year in Maya's, she can admit what it costs her (busy, then waiting).
- 2 to 3: Because the cost is named, she's ready to hear what she already did about it, and that each fix only held a month.
- 3 to 4: Because every fix worked, then came loose, and it wasn't her fault, she can accept a cause she never looked at:
  they were never joined.
- 4 to 5: Because the cause is "sewn on alone", she can accept that one more tactic, sewn on alone, won't fix it.
- 5 to 6: Because one more patch won't hold, she wants to know what does: the pieces in order, each sewn to the one before.
- 6 to 8: Because she now knows the order, she can believe the payoff scene: the new patch sewn to the last one holds.
- 8 to CTA: Because she understood it, she thinks of the founder friend whose year looks like Maya's.
Fixed points: cause (4) before the promise (5) before the mechanism (6); explanation before any claim; the brand (once,
end card) after everything. No proof, offer or guarantee rungs.

## 5. Question chain
- Master question: "Why was March full and April empty?" Opens on the text hook (0 s) and sung line 1; held through the
  fixes ("not cold email, not LinkedIn... then what?"); answered at the aha (about 40% in); relays to "then what holds?",
  answered by the mechanism and paid by the payoff scene.
- Re-hooks: the seam (about 4 s, "take Maya"), each failed fix killed (about 8 to 16 s), the spoken turn "Here's what
  nobody told her" (about 18 s), the chorus (about 22 s), the payoff replay (about 40 s).

## 6. Script bible (shared sheets; filled per line at the write step)
- **Speakers:** spine A: Voice 1, the narrator-singer, third person about Maya; the hook line is second person ("your
  lumpy months"), marked as the narrator speaking to the viewer, then "take Maya" switches to her story (row with its
  reason). The friend's question is framed ("Her friend asked her one thing:"). Spine B: each patch sings in "I",
  introduced by its own label on screen and its first words ("I'm the referral..."); the narrator takes the turn and
  the mechanism, marked by the spoken line.
- **Timeline:** March (full), April (quiet), "every slow month" (habit), the fixes in the order she tried them
  (referrals, cold email, LinkedIn, a coach and ads), then "this year" for the method; the payoff is the next April.
  No day counts or week counts.
- **Picture map:** her year = the quilt on the wall calendar · a month = a row of squares · a client = a filled square
  (no faces counted) · each fix (cold email, LinkedIn, ads, a coach) = a patch sewn on alone · a quiet month = a hole
  where a patch came loose · the cause = nothing sewn to anything · **the method's row:** the pieces sewn in order,
  each to the one before: the buyers' words (the first square), the offer (the square nobody else has), the weekly posts
  (the row that keeps adding), the page (the square where they say yes), ads last (sewn only onto squares that already
  held). It obeys the picture's rule (patches alone come loose; joined patches hold) and gives the method something to
  change (loose patches into one cloth).
- **Word list:** "lumpy" (her word; needs no explanation) · "Patchwork Marketing" (named in the chorus, the moment the
  picture is shown) · "offer nobody can compare" (explained in the same line) · "the page" (a page that turns them into
  calls). No platform names.
- **Setups and payoffs:** March full / April silent, set up in the hook, paid at the end with the next April holding ·
  the phone dark (set up scene 1, paid: it lights up? NO, that is a result; paid as the patch that holds) · "sewn on
  alone" (aha) paid by "sewn to the one before" (mechanism) · "waits on referrals" (stakes) paid by the referral patch
  now sewn into the cloth (never attacking referrals, EK-07).
- **Facts:** none. Maya is a composite in a stylized world; the song states no number, client, result or timeframe.
