## O60 (story tier): 136 words, about 54 s at 2.5 words/s (tier 45 to 75 s, STANDARDS 8)
```
Lumpy months? It was never where they came from.
Take Maya: referrals filled her March, then April went quiet.
Too busy in March to look for April.
So she tried fixes: cold email, LinkedIn, a coach, some ads.
Each one held a month, then came loose.
Then a friend with steady months asked her one thing:
"What's each fix sewn to?"
Nothing. Each was patched on alone.
That's Patchwork Marketing: pieces that never hold each other up.
Steady months don't need one more patch.
So she joined hers, in order:
her buyers' own words first, then an offer nobody else has,
posts that brought those buyers every week,
a page that booked the calls, and ads last, only on posts that worked.
Next April, the new patch held.
Send this to the founder whose March was full.
```
