## 3. Art direction
- Design read: calm, exact, premium. A consultant drawing on a dark board, not an ad.
- Palette by role: charcoal #1F1F1F ground; ivory #F8F6EF text and the path; gold #DD9A10 for people, key numbers and
  highlights; red #BE0F18 only for the leak (gap glow, the threads, the strike line); grey for lost or example matter.
  No red text on charcoal, no gold text on ivory.
- Type in motion: Montserrat 800 captions in phrase chunks, top third, spring rise (POP 320/23), hard cuts inside a
  line. Playfair Display 900 italic only for the month and PATH; Playfair numerals for the scoreboard.
- Layout: three zones. Captions 380-500; information zone 600-1020 (scoreboard, grid, tags, ledger, bubbles, CTA);
  the stage at y 1340 with labels at 1414. Nothing under 1520 except falling figures and the red glow.
- Signature device: the path with the gap. One camera move only: a 1.06 push into the gap on "the months go missing".
- Motion vocabulary: walk cycles at constant speed, gravity falls, spring pops, a squash-flip for March → April,
  numerals that fly down onto their planks.
- Texture: seeded grain 6%, soft vignette. Sound world: dry, small, wooden (clicks, taps, a low thump), one bed.
- Banned here: glow on figures, a redrawn logo, invented UI (the bubbles are labelled EXAMPLE and are plain shapes).
