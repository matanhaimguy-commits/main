## Script (about 108 words; the voice is the clock)
1. Your March: three clients. Your April: zero. Same LinkedIn.
2. Fifty-nine owners in our research describe this exact cycle.
3. The usual advice? Post more. Prospect more.
4. But your channel still works. The replies still come.
5. The months go missing after the reply.
6. Here's the three-step check.
7. One. Write down replies and new clients, for each of the last six months.
8. Two. Circle the months where replies held, but clients dropped. That gap is your leak.
9. Three. Today, answer every open reply with two real call times. Not "let me know."
10. Comment the word PATH, and we'll send you five ready-to-send messages for that step.
11. Fix the step, not the channel.
