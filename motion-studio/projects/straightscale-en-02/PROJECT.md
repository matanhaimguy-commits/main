## Why this idea (the five filters)
1. Bullseye signal: the hook IS a buyer's sentence: "2 years of posting content, running ads, and praying someone books a call." (VOC Q-E-0009, Trustpilot review, 20 Aug 2025). Also "content has been a black box for us for a long time" (Q-E-0183). PASS
2. Narrow problem (content that books no calls), not a narrow audience. PASS
3. Trinity of Value: A to B (guessing hooks to using the buyer's own sentences), unique (we show our own research, 567 real quotes), depictable (real quotes on paper, a highlighter lifts the words). PASS
4. New value: most people write hooks from their own head or copy viral templates; the take is "stop writing, go collect", with three concrete places and the rule "don't polish it". The proof is in the video itself: the hook that stopped the viewer was written by a buyer. PASS
5. One problem from video to lead magnet (the searches) to offer (Research, step 1 of the five). PASS
Rejected: "Your reel went viral. Zero calls." (only 2 buyer quotes mention views or virality: weak signal).

## Script (the voice is the clock)
1. Two years of posting content, running ads, and praying someone books a call.
2. We didn't write that. A business owner did, in a review.
3. It stopped you because it sounds like you. Your buyers stop for their own words.
4. Here are three places to find them.
5. One. Your competitors' worst reviews: what people paid for and never got.
6. Two. Reddit threads in your niche: the problem, before they know you exist.
7. Three. Newsletters where owners write about their worst month.
8. Copy the sentence. Don't polish it. Once it's polished, it sounds like an ad.
9. We collected five hundred sixty-seven of these before we posted a single video.
10. Comment WORDS for the exact searches we use.
