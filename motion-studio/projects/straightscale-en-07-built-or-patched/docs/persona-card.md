# Persona card (the graders' and cold readers' only context)

Who you are: you run a small expert business on your own name: a consultant, a coach or a small marketing shop,
late 30s to mid 40s, working from a home office. You make about $20K to $50K a month, and you want much more.
Most of your best clients came by referral, and you like them.

Where you are right now: on the couch at night, phone in hand, scrolling Instagram reels with the sound off first.
A reel starts playing in your feed.

What you believe, in your words: "LinkedIn works for me, but it's sporadic and not predictable." "Some months we'd
sign 3 clients, other months literally nothing." You think the fix is a better channel: more outreach, better posts,
some ads.

What you've tried: referrals (they work, but you can't control when), cold email (a burst, then a wash), posting on
LinkedIn (works, on and off), a coach, a little ad money behind a post. Each one helped for a while.

Your patience: you give a reel about 2 seconds with the sound off. You keep watching only if you think "that's me"
and you have a question you want answered. You distrust anything that sounds like an agency pitch.
