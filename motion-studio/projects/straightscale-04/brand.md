## Layout
- 9:16 safe zone: no key text in the top 250, bottom 400, right 140 px; left margin 96. Captions in the band y 290-570.
- 4:5: 64 px margins all round; captions in the band y 92-328.
- Ball anchor: y 980 (9:16), y 700 (4:5); the camera aims 0.22 s ahead of the ball.

## Voice and tone
Calm, direct, practical; short lines; teach, don't hype. No em dashes, no hype words, no numbers.
