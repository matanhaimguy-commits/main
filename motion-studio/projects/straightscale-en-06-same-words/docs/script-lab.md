## 4. Belief ladder (O60, spine A) and the because test
| Rung | Belief in her head | Installed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | "That's my page: people come from a post and it talks about something else." | the hook stack and line 2: the bubble against "solutions for growth" |
| 2 Stakes | "And I never see it; I just see a quiet month." | line 4, her moment |
| 3 Release | "More posts and ads didn't fail me; they sent people to the same page." | lines 5 and 6, each fix mocked, never her |
| 4 Aha | "The page uses jargon, the posts use my buyers' words: they don't match, so people fall through." | lines 8 to 10, THE PICTURE, named patchwork marketing |
| 6 Mechanism | "A matching page opens with the words my posts keep using, says what I sell in those words, asks for one thing." | lines 11 to 14 |
| 8 Payoff (process) | "Not everyone books, but nobody leaves because the page changed the subject." | line 15, the visitors' bubbles match the new page |
| CTA | "Part six is the ads question I already got wrong. Follow." | line 17 |

Because sentences:
- 1 to 2: Because she sees her own page in the grinning grey one, she can admit she never sees the people who leave.
- 2 to 3: Because the cost is a quiet month she can't explain, she's ready to hear that her fixes (more posts, ads) fed
  the same leak.
- 3 to 4: Because both fixes sent more people to the same page and it isn't her fault, she can accept a cause she never
  checked: the page and the posts speak different words.
- 4 to 6: Because the cause is "they don't match", she wants to know what a matching page says.
- 6 to 8: Because the page now uses the posts' words, she can believe the honest payoff: not everyone books, but nobody
  leaves confused.
- 8 to CTA: Because the reel defended her posts and named ads as the next question, she follows for part six.
Fixed points: cause (rung 4) before the mechanism (rung 6); the explanation before the payoff; the brand only on the end
card. The promise rung (5) rides in the hook's question ("then what fixes it?") and is not said as its own line: in
hidden_villain the villain cannot promise the fix that ends it, so its "You know what I'm scared of?" carries the hope.

## 6. Script bible (shared sheets; filled per line in `script.md`)
- **Speakers:** Voice 1 is the page, first person, talking to the viewer as "you" from line 1 ("I'm the page they land
  on") to line 16. Its mouth moves with every caption, so sound-off viewers see who speaks. Voice 2 is the narrator,
  one line, the CTA, on the end card. Maya never speaks; on screen she is "you" (seen from behind, or hands).
- **Timeline:** one present: the quiet month, the ads tried after it, the night she holds the post over the bin, then
  the new page. No day or week counts.
- **Picture map:** the posts = blue cloth printed with the buyers' words · a visitor = a clay professional in a blazer
  holding a bubble cut from the post's blue cloth · the old page = a stiff, shiny grey scrap with eyes and a grin,
  printed with jargon (SYNERGY, SOLUTIONS, LEVERAGE; never SCALE, which is in our handle: loop 4 sound-off note), stitched
  on at its corners with loose thread · patchwork marketing = the gap of loose threads between the blue post and the grey
  page, visitors falling through it · the offer = a blue piece stitched WHAT I SELL · the fix = a new page cut from the
  same blue cloth: headline in the posts' words, the WHAT I SELL piece, one BOOK A CALL button · the payoff = bubbles
  that match the headline; one visitor books, one walks away calmly · the CLIENTS wall organizer from the song = the
  quiet month (empty pockets, no faces counted).
- **Word list:** "page" (explained in line 1: "the page they land on") · "solutions for growth" (shown as the page's
  headline) · "your buyers' own words" (the series' term, set against "jargon" in the same line) · "patchwork marketing"
  (named in line 10 and explained in the same line: "page and post don't match") · "one offer" (line 14, paid by the
  WHAT I SELL piece). No platform names; "ads" never shown as a real ad tool (a round button with a coin).
- **Setups and payoffs:** "I change the subject" (line 1) paid by "the ones who leave, leave knowing what you sell"
  (line 15) · "solutions for growth" (line 2) paid by the new
  headline in the posts' words (line 11) · "the part that was doing its job" (line 6) paid by the posts staying and the
  page changing · the shrug past BOOK A CALL (line 3) paid by the one visitor who presses it (line 15) · the villain's
  fear (line 7) paid by "I'm out of a job" (line 16).
- **Facts:** none. Maya is a composite in a stylized world; the post headline is a story prop. The method line (page
  opens with the buyer words, shows the offer, takes the conversion) is offer sheet step 4 in the series' words.

generic checklist.
