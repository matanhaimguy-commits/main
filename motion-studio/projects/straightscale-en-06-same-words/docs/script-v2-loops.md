(the offer sheet says "a landing page that attracts the buyer, shows the offer and takes the conversions"; it does not
say one page per offer, per topic or per post), and every attempt to answer it in the voice became a critical fail;
(2) the approved text hook ("hit") and spoken hook ("land on") differ by one word, read together by every sound-off
reader; (3) the CTA's Voice 2 and its jump to ads; (4) the "said, not written" flags.
