# Hook stacks: StraightScale EN 06 "The page that changed the subject" (part five: Funnel)

Hook Lab H0 to H6 with `growth-engine/adapters/hook-lab-organic.md` (it wins over the skill for organic). Library:
`clients/straightscale/research/hook-library-en.md` (27 Oct 2026). Avatar AV-01, the lumpy-pipeline owner. Step taught:
Funnel (offer sheet step 4: the page the posts lead to says the offer in the same buyer words the post used, then takes
the conversion). Template `hidden_villain` (the villain is the mistake: a page that changes the subject; mock the fix,
never the owner). Format `reel_animation`, claymation, the song's cloth world. No picture is made in this stage, so
every grader read the H-01 shot spec in words (no concept frames, no image generation).

**How G-HOOK was run.** The Agent tool was not available in this session, so each blind grader was a fresh headless
Claude instance (`claude -p`, no tools, run from an empty scratch folder, no repo, no memory of other rounds). Each got
only the persona card below, a fixed grading task (the grade scale, "that's me", what it's about, keep watching sound
off, weakest thing) and per stack the text hook, spoken line 1 and the H-01 shot description. No brief, no angle, no
grades. Two new graders per round, five rounds, ten graders in all.

## Persona card (G-HOOK and every cold read)
From the song's `script-lab.md` section 2, with one sentence added for this step (their posts and bio link to a page
where people book a call; posts get reactions, bookings don't follow: Q-E-0009, Q-E-0183). The card says nothing about
the cause, the page fix or the method.
```
You run a founder-led expert business: a consultant, coach or small agency owner, late 30s to mid 40s, English-speaking,
about $20K to $50K a month, working from home. Your best clients came by referral and you like them. You post content
now and then, and your posts and bio link to a page on your site where people can book a call; some posts get good
reactions, but the bookings don't follow. It's late evening; you're on the couch, phone in hand, scrolling Instagram
Reels about business growth, sound off at first. A reel starts playing in your feed. What you believe, in your words:
"some months we'd sign clients, other months literally nothing"; "LinkedIn works for me, but it's sporadic and not
predictable"; you think you need a better channel, or more posts. What you've tried: cold email at higher volume,
LinkedIn, a coach, some ads; each helped for a while. What you don't know: how the separate parts of getting clients fit
together. Your doubts: you're tired of "post more", "more outreach", "fix your offer", and you distrust anything that
sounds like an agency pitch or "done-for-you". You give a reel 2 to 3 seconds and keep watching only while you have a
question you want answered and nothing confuses you.
```
(The song card's "3 clients" became "clients": no number on the card that could leak into a hook.)
