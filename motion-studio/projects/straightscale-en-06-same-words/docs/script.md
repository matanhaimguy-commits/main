## O60 final draft (loop 4): 172 words, about 69 s at 2.5 words/s (O60: 45 to 75 s)
Text hook (approved, 0 to 2.5 s): Your posts work until people hit your website
```
Your posts work until people land on your website.
Hi. I'm your website. I change the subject.
Your post was about hiring a first manager. I open with "We help you grow."
So they shrug and leave.
You never see it. You just see a slow month.
So you try ads, and pay to send them to me.
Then you start to blame your posts, the part that was working.
You know what I'm scared of?
A website that picks up where your post left off.
Your post talked about their problem. I talk about me.
So people get lost between your post and me.
A website that works starts with your post's question: "Hiring your first manager?"
Then it says what you sell, in your clients' words.
And it asks for one thing: book a call.
Not everyone books. But the ones who leave know what you sell.
And me, the old website? Out of a job.
Follow for the next part: when to pay for ads, and when not to.
```
