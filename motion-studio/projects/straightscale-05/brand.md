## Layout
- 9:16 safe zone: text and logo inside x 96-940, y 250-1520 (top 250, bottom 400, right 140 px kept clear).
- 4:5: margins x 92-988, y 104-1270 (feed post, no overlay UI).
- One left text edge for the whole film; the spinner centred in the safe band (x 518 on 9:16).

## Voice and tone
Calm, direct, practical; short lines; teach, don't hype. Never: prices, discounts, invented numbers, results, client
names, reviews, testimonials, guarantees, deadlines, "answer within 1 hour", "we chase your leads", hype words, em dashes.
