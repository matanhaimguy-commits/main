# Treatment · "Fat apo sistem" (week 43 post 3)

## 1 · Idea
A viral video is a spike; clients every month are a staircase. One red line carries the whole film, with the gold
client dot riding it; at the end the dot becomes the gold square of the real StraightScale mark.

## 3 · Art direction
- Ground charcoal #1F1F1F with a soft vignette and 6% grain (same world as reel 02 and the week's carousel).
- Type: Playfair Display 900 / 900 italic for lines (104-140 px), Montserrat 700-800 for labels (44 px, caps, +5 tracking).
  Ivory for statements, gold for the answer line of each beat. No red text on charcoal.
- Signature device: the red line (14 px #BE0F18 with a 4 px #E0414A core) and the gold dot (reel 02's client token).
- Motion: springs from lib/motion.js; masked rise for every line; camera is a smoothed target per beat (±0.25 s window).
- Banned here: fade-ins, glow, numbers on the chart (no axis values; month names are a calendar, not a claim).
