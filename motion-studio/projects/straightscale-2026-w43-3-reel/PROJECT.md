## Concepts (stage 4)
1. **Spike vs staircase (chosen).** One red line with the gold client dot riding it. A viral spike shoots out of frame and
   falls into a flat silence; then a staircase climbs one step per month, each step one part of the system; the pull-back
   shows the staircase end higher than the spike ever went; the dot becomes the gold square of the real mark.
   Survives every transition: the line and the dot. Message is physically in the image (a spike vs steps).
2. Lottery ticket vs calendar: a scratch card reveals nothing; a calendar fills a square per booking (echoes the
   carousel's last slide). Weaker: two objects, no single carry.
3. The S path as a conveyor: content goes in, clients come out every month. Close to reel 02's world; held back.
