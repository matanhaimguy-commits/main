## The mark
A red rounded square with an S-shaped channel cut through it (the channel is transparent, so the background
shows through) and a small gold square at the top right. The S channel reads as a path: this film uses it as
the client's path through a business. Never redraw the mark: the real PNG is used whenever the logo is shown;
any animated path is registered exactly on top of the real symbol and hands over to it.

## Layout (9:16)
Safe zone: text and logo out of the top 250 px, bottom 400 px, right 140 px. Margin 96 px.

## Light system (reel 03)
Ivory field (#F8F6EF), charcoal type, one accent: crimson #B31217 for text and fills on ivory. Gold only as small
filled marks, never gold text on ivory. Marble (#EDE4D6) and crimson only as out-of-focus background discs and rings.
Logo on ivory: `assets/brand/logo-full.png` (black wordmark).

## Voice and tone
Calm, direct, a strategist talking to an owner. Informal "ti". Short sentences. Teach, don't hype.
Never: prices, invented numbers, client names, testimonials, "we answer in 1 hour", "we chase your leads", em dashes,
hype words (revolutionary, game-changer, unlock).
