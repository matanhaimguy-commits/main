## Loop 1 (first draft): 81 words, about 32.4 s
```
Last quiet month, Maya paid to push me. She was guessing.
She wrote me at midnight, from her own head.
It wasn't me. She paid before any post was tested.
Now she cuts each post from her offer, built on one sentence a buyer said.
She posts them free, as tests, and watches which one the right buyers answer.
The money only goes behind that one.
And this time, it stayed.
Follow for part five: the page those buyers land on.
```
