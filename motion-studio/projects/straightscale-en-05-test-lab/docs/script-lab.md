## 2. Persona card (the cold readers' and hook graders' only input besides the script)
The song's card (`../straightscale-en-03-song/docs/script-lab.md` section 2), adjusted with two sentences this step
needs, marked here in bold; nothing in it names the cause, the test or the method.

You run a founder-led expert business: a consultant, coach or small agency owner, late 30s to mid 40s, English-speaking,
about $20K to $50K a month, working from home. Your best clients came by referral and you like them. **You post content
yourself when you find the time, and once or twice you put a little ad money behind a post to push it.** It's late
evening; you're on the couch, phone in hand, scrolling Instagram Reels about business growth, sound off at first. A reel
starts playing in your feed. What you believe, in your words: "some months we'd sign 3 clients, other months literally
nothing"; "LinkedIn works for me, but it's sporadic and not predictable"; **"content has been a black box for us for a
long time"**; you think you need a better channel or more content. What you've tried: cold email at higher volume,
LinkedIn, a coach, some ads; each helped for a while. What you don't know: how the separate parts of getting clients fit
together. Your doubts: you're tired of "post more", "more outreach", "fix your offer", and you distrust anything that
sounds like an agency pitch or "done-for-you". You give a reel 2 to 3 seconds and keep watching only while you have a
question you want answered and nothing confuses you. Would you send it, save it or follow?

Because sentences:
- 1 to 2: Because she sees her own quiet-month push, she can admit how it happened: written late, paid at once.
- 2 to 4: Because "paid before anyone saw me" is now on the table, she can accept that the mistake was the order, not the post.
- 4 to 6: Because a post is a guess until buyers answer it, she wants to know how to get that answer without paying.
- 6 to 8: Because she saw the free test pick one post, she can believe the payoff: the money backed the buyers' choice.
- 8 to CTA: Because the buyers now have somewhere to go (the door), she wants the next part: the page.
Fixed points: cause (L3) before the fix (L4 to L7); explanation before the claim; the brand only on the end card.
