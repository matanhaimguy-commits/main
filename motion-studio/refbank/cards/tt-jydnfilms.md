## Signature device
Hard black and white alternation as the rhythm. Almost every scene is either pure black with white elements or
pure white with black elements, and the cut between them is the beat (a white flash or a black frame on each
hit). Three recurring anchors carry the story through the flips: a phone with a text thread (the argument), a
faceted crystal heart (the feeling), and walls of one repeated word in solid plus outline type (the
obsession). Lyric words are treated as objects that break: "START" splits into "STA" and "RT" and smears; "hea"
completes to "heart" in a script; label chips like redacted tape hold small words. Colour appears only twice,
as signal: the blue message bubble and a green echo on the title word. The finale slows down with letters
lighting up one by one on a split-lit frame.

## Build it in our studio
Engine: HyperFrames for type, chat bubbles and flashes; canvas if the word walls need many moving rows.
1. Script 8-12 short lines; choose two anchors from the client's world (their app screen, their product).
2. Word walls: 6-8 rows of the key word in solid and outline styles, alternate rows sliding opposite ways.
3. Hits: split words on the beat (two spans that fly apart with a motion smear), white or black frame flashes
   on strong beats from beats.py.
4. Anchors: the client's real app or product photo cut out; any phone UI must be the real product, never an
   invented screen. A crystal or chrome object can come from `gen-image` (paid, approval) or the registry.
5. Finale: one line lighting up letter by letter on a split-lit frame; small real logo on black.
- Hard part: keeping two-tone discipline and phone-size legibility (the tiny paragraph texture is decoration
  only; every readable word stays at 44 px or more).
- Difficulty 3; render about 16 s per second in HyperFrames.
