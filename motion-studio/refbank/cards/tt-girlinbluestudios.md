## Signature device
A keyed host over a full-bleed concept board. The host is cut out (green screen) and parked in the lower left,
about 55% of the frame width and 40% of its height, never moving position, so the background can change freely
behind them. Each background is one full-bleed image for one brand pillar, held 3-7 s while the host explains
it: name, product, setting, campaign, slogan, community challenge. Each pillar's key words land once as a
centred title in an outlined display serif (white or yellow fill, thick black stroke, about 90 px on a 1080
frame). The structure is a build-up from name to a whole marketing plan, which makes the idea feel complete.

## Why it works
The contrarian idea (a soap that celebrates dirt) is the real hook: it is surprising and easy to retell. The
"if I were" format shows the creator's thinking as a free sample of their service. Every 4-6 s a new layer of
the brand arrives, so the idea keeps growing until it feels like a real launch. The closing "would you buy
this?" is a direct comment trigger, and the campaign images invite debate.

## Build it in our studio
Engine: client footage of the host (a founder or strategist filmed against green or a plain wall), assembled
in HyperFrames.
1. Film the host in one take, framed for a lower-left cut-out; key with the background-removal tool.
2. Backgrounds: the agency's real concept boards, mockups or photography, one per pillar. If concept images must
   be made, `gen-image` (paid, approval) with a fixed style prompt for consistency, labelled as concept work.
3. Titles: one outlined display font, popped on the exact word in the transcript (HyperFrames captions timed
   from the transcription).
4. Add a slow 3-5% push on each background so the holds do not feel static, and keep text clear of the top 250
   px and bottom 400 px.
- Hard part: the idea, not the build. The concept must be genuinely surprising and the host must be good on
  camera. Build cost is low (talking head plus stills, HyperFrames render about 16 s per second of film).
