## Signature device
A keyed host over a full-bleed concept board. The host cut-out sits in the lower left (about 55% of frame width,
40% of height) and never moves, so the background can change freely. Each background is one full-bleed image
for one brand pillar, held 3-7 s while the host explains it: name, product, setting, campaign, slogan, community
challenge. Each pillar's key words land once as a centred title in an outlined display serif (white or yellow,
thick black stroke, about 90 px on a 1080 frame). The build-up from name to full marketing plan makes the idea
feel complete.

## Why it works
The contrarian idea (a soap that celebrates dirt) is surprising and easy to retell. The "if I were" format is a
free sample of the creator's service. A new brand layer arrives every 4-6 s, so the idea keeps growing. "Would
you buy this?" is a direct comment trigger.

## Build it in our studio
Engine: client footage of the host (founder or strategist) assembled in HyperFrames.
1. Film one take framed for a lower-left cut-out; remove the background locally.
2. Backgrounds: the agency's real boards, mockups or photos, one per pillar; if images must be made,
   `gen-image` (paid, approval) with one fixed style prompt, labelled as concept work.
3. Titles in one outlined display font, timed to the transcript words.
4. A slow 3-5% push on each background; keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px.
- Hard part: the idea and the host's delivery, not the build. Difficulty 2, low render cost.
