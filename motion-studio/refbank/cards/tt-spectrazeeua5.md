## Signature device
A second-person build-along on one site. Each clause adds one physical layer to the same patch of ground, seen from
a high 3/4 or overhead camera, so every addition reads as a change to the last picture. The geometry is primitive:
boxes for pits and walls, a plane for water, a cone for the mound, a tiled floor and a few props. Objects pop or
extrude in while the camera drifts. The picture often leads the words by 2-4 s; the water shows before "flooded".
Captions are 2-3 lowercase words in white bold sans with a dark edge, at about 62% of frame height.
