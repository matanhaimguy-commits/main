viewer sees the cheap set (rug, light stand, wrinkled cloth). Act 2 shows the environment assembling in about
four stages, each held about 0.75 s, from bare geometry to full layout in viewport colours. The bridge is the
key move: the original green-screen frame reappears full screen with the new world peeking at its edges, then

the final shot. The final render is held for 5.5 s with a strong value structure: a dark foreground canopy
framing a bright, hazy middle ground where the tiny figure sits, which reads as scale and calm.
