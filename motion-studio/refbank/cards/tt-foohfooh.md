## Signature device
An escalation ladder of persuasion tactics played by one character in one shot. Each beat is a new tactic, and
each tactic is more absurd than the last: bribe, guilt, fear, loyalty, money logic, FOMO, peer obligation,
fake scarcity. The camera never moves (phone at arm's length, eye level, head and shoulders filling the lower
two thirds), and jump cuts remove every breath so arguments land back to back. Each new tactic gets a new face
(wide eyes, side glance, whisper, grin) as the visual "cut" between beats. A fixed headline in the upper third
names the persona, so every line is read as "that friend" rather than as the creator.
