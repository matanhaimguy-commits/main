## Signature device
One recurring photoreal character in one fixed location who acts out every sentence literally, with each
theory given its own visual transformation of him: body swaps with a light-streak glitch for "other versions",
a grid of nine variants split by glowing lines for "parallel universes", a costume change and vertical light
seam for "past life", an anatomical cutaway for "the brain". The props (mailbox, butterfly) appear in every
version, so the viewer always knows it is the same moment. Captions are 1-3 words, white bold sans with a soft
shadow at about 75% height, word-synced to the voice.
