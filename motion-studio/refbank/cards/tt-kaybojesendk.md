## Signature device
The product itself, scaled to roughly 12 m, placed in a real landmark location in the brand's home city, and
signed by an in-world object instead of a graphic. Two birds in the brand's real colours (matte sage body, cream
head, black eye), one large and close, one about half size and further back, give depth and a sense of family.
They sit low in the water with a believable waterline and faint reflection. The logo never appears as an overlay:
it is on a small floating buoy that the camera arcs toward, so the brand is the last thing read. A handheld or
gimbal walk arcs about 90 degrees around the big bird for parallax, then holds.
