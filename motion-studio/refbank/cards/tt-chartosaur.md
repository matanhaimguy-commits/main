## The film in one line
A voice promises "how to impress your boss", shows a rainbow-coloured executive dashboard, names a three-letter
rule (DAL), and rebuilds that same dashboard one letter at a time: strip the colour and axes, add
previous-period comparisons, then reshuffle the tiles so the key numbers sit top left.

## Signature device
One "before" dashboard that is visibly repaired in place, rule by rule, with a giant acronym card as the
chapter marker between repairs. Layout on a 1080 x 1920 frame: the dashboard occupies the top 45% (title with a
black rule, a 3 x 3 tile grid); captions sit centred at about 52% height; the lower 40% stays empty page.
Each rule follows the same four-step loop: (1) acronym card: the dashboard blurs and the letters D, A, L stack
vertically on the left at about 200 px tall, white with a black outline, the active letter turns yellow and
the rest of its word types out from it in smaller yellow caps; (2) zoom one tile forward out of the blur as a
large card; (3) apply the fix to that tile in two or three visible steps (rainbow to two tones to navy, axis
removed; plain number to number plus green delta); (4) cut back to the whole dashboard with the fix applied to
every tile. The final rule is shown as motion only: tiles slide to new grid positions.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: `hyperframes` (real HTML/SVG charts, CSS blur filter on a seeked timeline, GSAP Flip-style tile
  reflow; search the registry for chart and caption blocks first).
- Scene plan: 1) open on a client-relevant hook still or line, no third-party meme; 2) build the "before"
  dashboard from the client's real product UI; 3) acronym card component with an accent letter and typed
  suffix; 4) per rule: blur, zoom one tile, restyle in 2-3 steps, show the whole; 5) last rule as a tile
  reflow; 6) word-by-word captions synced to VO timestamps.
- Hard part: the restyle steps must read as one object changing, not a cut. Tween fills, opacity of axes and
  gridlines separately and keep each bar's geometry fixed.
- Difficulty 2. VO needs ElevenLabs (keys in .env); about 32 s at roughly 16 s render per second.
