## Signature device
Underestimated, then the drop. Six seconds of lo-fi phone selfies and a quoted dismissive line, then a hard cut to
black on the audio's punchline, then the polished portfolio at full speed. The two halves are as different as
possible in craft: handheld, warm, natural selfies versus saturated, graphic title work. The reel half uses one
transition grammar throughout: the outgoing shot is smeared into vertical colour bars (a pixel-stretch or
slit-scan glitch, bars 2 to 10% of frame width, about 0.3 to 0.5 s), and the next shot resolves out of the bars.
Each portfolio clip lasts 1 to 2 s, and the persistent caption keeps the joke readable during the flex.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: HyperFrames for the edit, captions and the glitch transitions (search the registry for glitch,
  chromatic aberration and pixel-stretch effects before building); client footage for the setup selfies and the
  client's own portfolio clips for the reel.
- Scene plan: 1) client films two 2 to 3 s selfies with a quoted line about being underestimated; 2) cut to
  black with the punchline caption; 3) six to eight portfolio clips of 1 to 2 s each, cut to the beat of a
  licensed or generated track; 4) one glitch transition preset reused for every cut; 5) end mid-glitch on the
  strongest piece.
- Hard part: the audio. We cannot use the trend sound in a client deliverable unless the client posts it with the
  platform's own sound; otherwise build a punchline moment with a generated track and a silence-before-hit.
- Difficulty 2: an edit of client material. Render cost about 16 s per second of film in HyperFrames.
