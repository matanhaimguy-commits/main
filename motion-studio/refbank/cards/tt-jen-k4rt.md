## Signature device
The assembly speed-build with a top-to-bottom reveal. One tall artefact (about 1:3) is made in a fixed order,
top section first, so each new section appears just below the last and the recording's viewport keeps sliding
down the page. Every 1-2 s there is a jump cut to the next action (search, place, restyle, type), and every few
cuts the recording zooms into the page so the viewer can read the newest section at near full width. The final
2 s pull back to show the whole artefact for the first time, then swap to a clean presentation frame: the
finished poster centred, about 70% of frame width, on a contrasting patterned background. The order is the
reward: the viewer has seen every section once in close-up and only sees them together at the end.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: `footage` (a real screen recording of the client's own tool or file) cut and framed in
  `hyperframes`; or a pure `hyperframes` build of the client's real asset assembling itself, without any app
  chrome (we never invent a fake interface).
- Scene plan: 1) title line over the blank canvas, 1 s; 2) 10-14 shots of 1-2 s, each one adding a section
  top to bottom, with a digital zoom on every third; 3) pull back to the full asset; 4) presentation frame on
  a branded pattern, held 2 s.
- Hard part: legibility of small UI at phone size. Crop in hard (2-3 x) on the action and keep the overlay
  title at 80 px plus.
- Difficulty 2. Music must be licensed or generated (not the original song).
