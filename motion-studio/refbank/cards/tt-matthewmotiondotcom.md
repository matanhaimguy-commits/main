## Signature device
The screen-in-frame creator format: the ad is never shown full screen. It plays inside an editing app on a desk
monitor, filmed by a phone from a seat in front of it, with a coloured LED wall panel in the top third and a
two-line white-box caption ("POV: you make animations for brands for fun pt. N") fixed across the middle third
for the whole video. That framing turns a spec ad into a personal story (a creator doing brand work for fun) and
a series. Inside, the ad follows a classic matchup grammar: tease (glitch slices), the day ("THIS SUNDAY"), side
A (player plus logo), side B, VS split, a hero object macro (the ball), call to action ("TUNE IN LIVE"), date,
master logo. Cuts every 0.5 to 1 s, two-colour duotone (each team's colour) for the glitch and ball sections.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: HyperFrames for the promo (registry glitch, RGB-split and light-leak effects; GSAP for type slams),
  then `footage`: play the render on a real monitor and film it, or use it full screen if no wrapper is wanted.
- Scene plan: 1) script the 8-beat matchup with the client's real event (two acts, two teams, two products);
  2) build each card as a sub-composition with one duotone per side; 3) glitch transitions every 0.5 s in the
  tease, hard cuts on the beat after that; 4) VO in ElevenLabs, music ducked under it; 5) film the playback on a
  monitor with a fixed caption if it is for the agency's own channel.
- Hard part: cut-out photos of real people need clean mattes and matching grade; use the client's own photos
  and our remove-background tool, then a shared duotone to hide mismatches.
- Difficulty 3: all 2D and in our stack; the work is in the cut-outs and timing to the VO.
