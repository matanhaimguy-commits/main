## Signature device
The screen-in-frame creator format: the ad is never full screen. It plays inside an editing app on a desk
monitor filmed by a phone from the chair, with a coloured LED panel in the top third and a two-line white-box POV
caption fixed across the middle third for the whole video. That framing turns a spec ad into a personal story and
a series. Inside, the ad follows classic matchup grammar: tease (glitch slices), the day, side A (player plus
logo), side B, VS split, a hero object macro (the ball), call to action, date, master logo. Cuts every 0.5 to 1 s,
a two-colour duotone (each team's colour) for the glitch and ball sections.
