her, a set of fangs many times larger rises out of the dark around the whole frame and snaps shut on both, and
the last shot is a point of view looking down past yellow fins at another lure and another mouth rising from
below.

## Signature device
A three-step size ladder where each predator is revealed by being framed by a bigger one. Step 1: the diver
alone, about 15% of frame height. Step 2: the anglerfish, about 3 times her size, introduced at the frame edge
and held in a calm two-shot for 7 s so the threat feels like the whole story. Step 3: the true scale arrives
without a cut: soft, defocused fangs (each about as tall as the anglerfish) rise into the frame margins from
below so the frame itself becomes the inside of a mouth, then close in 0.5 s with heavy motion blur. Step 4 turns
the ladder on the viewer: a hard cut to POV, own fins in the foreground at the frame corners, and the next lure
and mouth far below in the dark. Depth is sold by fog: everything fades to deep blue with distance, so scale
reads from haze, not detail.
