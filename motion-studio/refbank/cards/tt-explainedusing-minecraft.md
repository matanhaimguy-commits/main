## The film in one line
In a blocky game world, Richard claims all the land around spawn on day one, latecomer Paul has to farm it for
half his harvest and three free days a week in exchange for protection from zombies, a zombie plague kills half
the farmers so Paul can finally demand payment in emeralds, and a billboard map of Europe in 1347 reveals it
was the real story of feudalism and the Black Death.

## Signature device
Metaphor world with named stand-ins. Every abstract role is one named character with a fixed visual tag (the
lord: sunglasses, chain, later a crown and throne; the peasant: default look plus a floating name tag), and
every rule is a game mechanic the audience already knows (claiming land, mobs at night, emeralds as money).
Text inside the world (signs, a map billboard) carries labels instead of overlays. One cut per phrase, one
word of caption at a time (white bold sans with a dark shadow, about 70 px on 1080, centred mid-frame).

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: `canvas` with our own isometric or blocky world drawn with projection math (no game assets), or a
  flat 2D board-game world; captions and timing in `hyperframes`.
- Scene plan: 1) pick a world the client's audience knows (a supermarket, a football league, a lemonade stand);
  2) two named characters with one visual tag each; 3) script in the ladder above, about 160 words;
  4) 6-8 reusable sets, 40+ short shots built from them; 5) word captions from VO timings.
- Hard part: shot count. 45 shots in 70 s is a lot of staging; build a small set kit and reuse angles.
- Difficulty 4 for a voxel-style world, 3 for a flat 2D version.
