## Signature device
A compatibility matrix redrawn as a physical plumbing diagram. One source (the bag) sits top centre with a
title above it. A single trunk drops from the bag's port down the vertical centre line and splits into eight
symmetrical curved branches, four per side, each ending at a wrist with a white tape band. Recipients are a
2 x 4 grid of arms entering from the frame edges at a slight upward angle: left column positive types, right
column negative, rows top to bottom AB, B, A, O. Closed fist means "no", open palm with the type written on it
in white bold sans means "yes". Each beat lasts about 3.6 s: 1 s with the new label and all fists closed
(tubes drained), then the compatible branches fill dark red and their fists unfold, then a 2 s hold. The order
is chosen so the count of open hands climbs 1, 2, 2, 4, 2, 4, 4, 8 and the last beat (universal donor) fills
the whole tree. The camera never moves; only the label, the tube colour and the hands change.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: `hyperframes` (SVG paths with stroke-dashoffset fills, GSAP timeline) or `canvas` for hand-drawn
  receivers.
- Scene plan: 1) design the source object and 8 receiver icons with two states each (closed/open), in the
  client's brand; 2) draw the branching tree as SVG paths from one trunk; 3) for each option, swap the label,
  drain all paths, then animate the compatible paths' fill from trunk to tip over 0.4 s and switch the
  receivers' state with a 0.3 s spring; 4) hold 2 s; 5) final option fills all.
- Hard part: receivers that feel tactile without 3D. Use illustrated two-pose icons with a squash on the state
  change; fill paths with a moving gradient head so the flow reads as liquid.
- Difficulty 2: all vector work, about 30 s at roughly 16 s render per second in HyperFrames.
