## Signature device
**One prop, two paths.** The same everyday object goes down two paths side by side: resell it as is, or transform it.
The prop never changes, only what each person does with it, so the difference is the whole lesson. The numbers are
part of the story's example ($2 in, $4 out), not a claim.
