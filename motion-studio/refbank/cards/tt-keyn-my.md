She extends the phone at arm's length straight at the lens, screen facing us, playing the opening of the next
shot (her in the white studio). The camera keeps pushing until the phone screen covers about 70% of the frame
at 4.5 s, then cuts to the same studio clip full frame, so it reads as flying into the phone. After the portal
the film becomes a tight product demo: every shot is hands on the product, about 0.5 to 1 s each, closer each
time (rack, jar, lid macro), then a second transition carried by the product itself: she picks up a jar and the
camera follows it off the table to the shelf where it lives.

## Build it in our studio
- Engine: client footage for a live version (a phone, a gimbal and the products); HyperFrames to cut and add
  the portal composite. A stylised version works in the canvas engine with client product photos: a flat phone
  shape in an illustrated room, screen playing the next scene, scale-through transition.
- Scene plan: 1) symmetric wide of the client's space with one prop waiting; 2) person enters, sits, holds the
  phone at the lens; 3) replace the phone screen with the first demo shot (corner pin on the phone, track 4
  points) and scale it up until it fills the frame; 4) five to eight hands-on close-ups of 0.5 to 1 s; 5) carry
  the product to its final place, hold 1 s.
- Hard part: the push into the screen. Filming the screen live gives moire; better to composite the real clip
  into a green or black phone screen and animate the zoom ourselves.
- Difficulty 2: an easy edit with client footage. Render cost small.
