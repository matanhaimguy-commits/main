## Signature device
One literal prop per spoken phrase, joined by focus pulls. Each beat is a single centred composition on white. The outgoing prop blurs out while the incoming one blurs in from the same radius to sharp (roughly 0.2 s, radius in the tens of px on a 1080 frame), sometimes with a small push so the blur carries motion; there are no hard cuts. One accent colour marks the key word in every phrase, a serif italic marks the emotional words, and tiny fine print sits under headlines as texture only. Large soft out-of-focus accent rings drift at the edges so the white field feels like space, not paper.

## Why it works
- A claim most marketers half-believe already: relatable and quotable.
- Each phrase is literally illustrated, so the message survives sound-off.
- Constant small novelty with no hard cuts feels calm but never stale.
- The growing pill cloud rewards staying; the circled last line is a clear ending and a save trigger.
- Faceless format: the edit is the creator's portfolio.

## Steal this (grammar) / Leave this (content)
- Steal: a prop per phrase; focus pulls as the only transition; one accent colour plus a serif italic accent; fine print as texture; a growing cloud as the long hold; a circled final line.
- Leave: the green, the script and its wording, the person pictograms, the floating review cards, the creator's mark and handle, any music.

## Build it in our studio
Engine: canvas (blur radius as a spring per layer, subframe motion blur on the hand); HyperFrames works too, but heavy blur renders slowly in software.
1. Write and time the voiceover (client voice or ElevenLabs; ask first).
2. One prop per phrase, drawn in code and specific to the client's message.
3. A shared focus-pull transition for every beat.
4. Drifting bokeh rings.
5. Typed closing line with a hand-drawn ellipse; brand sting.
Hard part: props that are specific rather than generic icons (our look rules ban generic avatars and fake UI). Difficulty 2. Native 9:16: keep props in the middle band and accent words at 44 px or more.
