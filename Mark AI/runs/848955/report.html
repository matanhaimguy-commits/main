Why the empty GB result is real, not a coverage artifact

I ran list_geo_countries before spending anything on a geo read. GB is covered with 1,063,783 ads in the index — the second-largest country after the US. An uncovered country code returns empty results rather than an error, which would have faked this entire finding. It did not.

Of the 8 GB rows returned, the advertisers were a practitioner-training PDF and a migraine advertorial. Zero brands are advertising a TMJ relief device to Britain. Meanwhile the generic unit is already stocked and retailing in the UK — Amazon.co.uk B0DGPYSRS4 (Zaybuon, 50 g), uk.voghion.com at £49.54 and £33.35, ninelife.uk at £70.88. Demand infrastructure exists; nobody is running direct response against it.

Copy test. yourTMJ names its own constraint: small-batch US manufacture priced to ration demand at maximum capacity. A supply-capped US manufacturer cannot open a UK front quickly — no £ pricing, no UK fulfilment, no UKCA route, and a compliance frame (FSA/HSA) that means nothing here. This is an observed constraint from their own page, not "they're too big to care".

Secondary wedges: ANGLE — the "your night guard was never designed to stop the clenching" mechanism frame is entirely unclaimed in GB. OFFER — incumbent sells a single pen with no ladder.

How we win

WHO: the clencher who wakes with a headache and has already cracked a tooth, and the appliance-exhausted veteran of a decade of splints. WHY NOW: it recurs every single night and the damage is cumulative and visible at the dentist. WHAT: a rechargeable heated massage pen for the masseter and temporalis, heat plus vibration, precision tip. WHY THIS: the plausible mechanism is already validated in-market — a night guard protects teeth but does nothing to stop the muscle clenching, and consistent daily heat with self-massage is the thing that addresses the muscle rather than the enamel. WHY US: the one operator proving the money is US-only and has publicly declared itself supply-capped and small-batch; it has no £ pricing, no UK fulfilment and a US-specific compliance frame, so a British front is not something it opens next month. WHICH ROUTE: native story led 80/20, per the Step-8 arithmetic. HOW WE SELL: long-form first-person native — the years of failed appliances, then the mechanism, then the object — into a mechanism-led PDP, with a gel-pack bump and a second-unit OTO. WHY ECONOMICS: £29.83 GP at 73.1% margin and 3.71× gives a £31.62 breakeven CPA and 4.22 test orders/day on a £4,000 month — fundable, provided landed cost lands under about £13.60.

Risk flags

  • LOAD-BEARING ASA/CAP. This must be advertised as a wellness and comfort tool. No "treats TMJ", no "relieves TMD", no therapeutic claim. yourTMJ's own hedging — "a general wellness tool… not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease" — is the template to copy, and CAP is stricter than the FTC on health claims.
  • LOAD-BEARING Product safety. This is a heated lithium-battery handheld reaching 118°F against the face. UKCA/CE conformity, battery documentation and a named UK responsible person are obligations, not paperwork. The fast-kill list flags fire/electrical liability for good reason — the supplier must produce conformity docs before you buy inventory. Verify this at the same time as the source.
  • Economics. Gate breaks above ~£13.60 landed.
  • Media. Unprofitable at £1.40 CPC on every modelled CVR.
  • Concentration. The entire money-proof rests on one operator. If yourTMJ pauses, the category read changes.
