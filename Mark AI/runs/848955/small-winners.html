Why your two filters fight each other

This is the real finding, and it is worth more than any single product on the list.

50–200 active ads11% pass
150–500 active ads43% pass

Ad count measures spend. Traffic measures revenue. In the 50–200 band — the one you called best — only 2 of 18 brands cleared 70K visits. Almost everything in that band sits between 5,000 and 50,000 visits a month, which is a £5–40K business, not a £50–100K one.

The band where the two criteria actually coexist is 200–500 ads. But that band is populated by real companies — Takomo, Bogey Bros, xTool, Portronics, Avocado Green Mattress — not small competitors you can outflank. That is the trade-off, stated plainly: small enough to beat and big enough to prove £50–100K/month are close to mutually exclusive.

The metric that actually finds outliers

Visits per active ad. It identifies brands making unusual revenue on unusual efficiency, which is exactly the profile worth copying. The sweep median is roughly 220. Artevive runs 2,056 — nine times the median, on the smallest ad count in the whole set. That is the single most interesting number on this page.

What actually qualifies

Three brands clear 70K/month, sell a problem-solving physical product, and are not a supplement or a claims minefield. I am not padding this to ten.

1 · Artevive — under-helmet Bluetooth moto earbuds

arteviveearplugs.com · brand id 7496221 · ad 151986988 · GB-targeted
visits 102,829/moads 50 per ad 2,056live 199 days price $99.99UK share 15.4% status out of stock

The standout of the entire exercise, and the only brand that survived both the traffic bar and the original 50–200 ad band. Fifty ads producing 100K visits means the creative is doing the work, not the budget — which is the opposite of most of this list. The pain is exact and felt on every ride: “If your helmet speakers hurt, they don't belong in your ride.” Motorcyclists already spend £150+ on comms kit, so the ticket is unremarkable to them. At 2% and £75 that is roughly £150K/month.

2 · Ozzi Gear — lightweight packable camping chairs

ozzigear.com · 422 active ads · Health/Home niche · GB-targeted
visits 172,583/motrend 80K → 139K → 173K UK share 44.8%ads 422 price $116claim 100,000+ customers

The strongest trajectory in the sweep — traffic more than doubled across three months — and the only qualifying brand where the UK is the largest market. The positioning is a clean problem statement: “Say goodbye to heavy, bulky & awkward to use outdoor gear.” Lifetime warranty and a 45-day guarantee are doing the risk reversal. At $116 the margin structure is comfortable. The catch is that 422 ads is a genuinely well-funded operator, not a soft target.

Clears the bar and sells a real problem-solving product — fluoride-free hydroxyapatite toothpaste for sensitivity and remineralisation, which is a genuine and growing category. But the trend is the wrong way (down 38% in two months), the price point is low for paid acquisition, and the UK is 1.6% of its traffic. Included for completeness; I would not start here.

Three that clear 70K but I would not build on

Kalm (112,307/mo) — magnetic therapy jewellery. Commercially real, with 15,000+ reviews. But the entire proposition rests on magnet therapy claims, and the ASA would take that apart in the UK. Naturva (97,515) and Healthy Petz (156,464) — both ingestibles running aggressive health advertorials; Naturva markets to COPD sufferers. Takomo and Bogey Bros clear the bar easily but are established brands with their own manufacturing, not products to copy.

The filter I would use next

Rather than picking an ad band and hoping, invert it: sweep wide on ads, then let traffic do the filtering. That is what produced the only two names on this page worth acting on.

STEP 1 — cast wide on GetHookd
  active_ads_count 100, active_ads_count_max 800
  run_time 150, status active, geo GB
  niche "16,23,17,4,26,27"          // no apparel, no supplements
  page_type "product_page,advertorial,listicle"
  performance_scores "winning"
  ads_per_brand_limit 1, limit 20   // page through 3-4 pages

STEP 2 — traffic-gate every domain
  apify: tri_angle/fast-similarweb-scraper   ($0.002/domain)
  keep visits >= 70,000/mo

STEP 3 — rank on VISITS PER ACTIVE AD, not on ad count
  > 800  = outlier efficiency, investigate first
  200-800 = normal
  < 150  = spending hard for little return; skip

STEP 4 — then apply the product screen
  physical . problem-solving . not ingestible . not apparel
  . GBP29-80 band . dropshippable

Step 3 is the part that was missing. Ad count alone told me Nord Labs was the best target on the list; visits-per-ad would have flagged Artevive instead, which is the one that actually holds up.
