## 2. The model-drawn logo problem and its fix

- Problem (Failure Library FL-13, found 15 Oct): prompts that asked for the logo got a lookalike from the image model
  (wrong shape, wrong red, wrong type). The brand kit says real logo files only. It showed on the old style reference
  and on the drafts.
- Prevention: prompts leave the bottom-left corner calm and empty; after review the real file is pasted with
  `growth-engine/tools/brand_overlay.py`. The real files are in `clients/straightscale/brand/`. A new style reference
  (STYLE-TOP v1, a crop without the logo) passed its review with zero defects (0f05a35).
- Repair tool for images made before the rule: `growth-engine/tools/fix_drawn_logo.py` finds the drawn wordmark and
  mark in the bottom-left zone, inpaints those pixels and pastes the real logo. Its output is a new version with its
  own review.
- Side effect found by the reviewers: the inpaint also erased nearby details (bead highlights, a desk edge). The v3
  code edits restored the original pixels there.
- Not fixable: the carousel live since 15 Oct still shows a drawn logo and cannot be edited after posting. Owner
  decision queued (LIVE-CAROUSEL-LOGO, section 5).
