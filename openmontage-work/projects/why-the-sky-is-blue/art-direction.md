# Art direction — "Sunlight, Sorted by Color"

**Design read:** curious-science short for a general audience. Make invisible light physics feel
tactile and luminous: calm wonder, not hype.

**Taste dials:** visual_variance 5 · motion_intensity 5 · information_density 3.

## Palette (taken from the subject itself)
- Space: `#05081A` / night navy `#0B1430` / indigo `#141B4D`
- Day sky: cerulean `#3B8BEB` → light `#8FD0FF`
- Sun: `#FFE7A8` core, `#FFD27A` glow
- Sunset: amber `#FF9A4A`, rose `#F2617A`, dusk `#2B2D6E`
- Light itself (the only saturated accents): red `#FF4D4D`, orange `#FF9A3C`, yellow `#FFE15A`,
  green `#4CD787`, blue `#3B8BEB`, violet `#9B6BFF`
- Text: warm off-white `#F5F2EA`

## Type
- Fredoka (rounded, friendly) for the few labels and the closing title.
- Nunito for subtitles.

## Motion character
Soft springs (damping ~14), slow-in/slow-out, waves always gently travelling (light is never
static). One kinetic accent only: the blue splash.

## Signature device
**The blue splash**: a blue wave hitting a molecule and bursting outward in rings and arrows
(sc4). Echoed once in sc7 as blue fragments peeling away from the sunset beam. Nowhere else.

## Scene inventory (each scene has its own primary subject)
| Scene | Primary subject | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| sc1 | Sun + Earth in black space, white beam | extreme wide, horizontal |
| sc2 | White beam fanning into a stack of six spectral waves | medium, stacked bands |
| sc3 | Field of N₂/O₂ molecules at their own scale | wide, layered depth |
| sc4 | One molecule: red passes, blue splashes, "×5" | close-up, centered |
| sc5 | A person on a hill under a filling blue sky | wide landscape |
| sc6 | Violet puzzle: sky window + sunlight bars + eye curve | split layout |
| sc7 | Sunset: long light path, blue peeling away | wide, low horizon, warm |
| sc8 | Closing typography with spectral underline sorting | centered type |

## Anti-patterns avoided
Generic AI-purple gradients, text-only slides, stock rainbow clip-art, dense labels.
Captions are accessibility subtitles; they are hidden in sc8 where the same words are on screen.
