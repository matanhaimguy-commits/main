DELIVERABLE: One finished raster advertisement at exactly 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350 px), filling the whole canvas edge to edge. No mockup, no device frame around the ad, no browser frame, no border, no page number, no production labels, no watermark.

AUDIENCE AND MOMENT: A US woman, roughly 35 to 60, who paid someone to build an online store and was later locked out of it, or left to finish it herself. She is product aware and suspicious of every store-building offer. The moment: she recognises her own lock-out line, then sees the two places her last build went wrong, and sees that this offer answers both. One main point: check whose name the store is in, and who works on it after launch day. Awareness: product aware. Angle: the burned stores went wrong in two checkable places. Target belief: not every builder is the same; the ones that burned people kept the store in their own account and stopped on launch day.

READING ORDER (make it obvious through size and position): 1) hook, full width at the top; 2) subhead directly under it; 3) the two column headers, left then right; 4) comparison row 1 (row label on the seam, left cell, right cell); 5) comparison row 2; 6) body text under the right column header area; 7) proof line across the full width near the bottom; 8) the CTA button at bottom right.
