NATIVE STYLE (read first): organic content, not a designed ad. A plain online news-article view that someone might screenshot and share. DELIVERABLE: one finished raster image at exactly 1:1 (1080 x 1080 px), filling the canvas. No device frame, no browser chrome, no border.

Under it, the standfirst in a lighter grey sans over two or three lines. Under that, one short grey placeholder bar where a byline would be (no readable text). The lower half is one wide candid photograph, edge to edge: a kitchen table at night; an overstuffed manila folder of printed receipts with a few sheets fanned out; a closed silver laptop beside it; a mug of cold tea; a phone lying face down; warm light from a single lamp off to one side; shot from standing height on a phone, slightly off-level, natural shadows, a little grain. Text sizes: headline about 6 percent of canvas height per line, standfirst about 3.2 percent.
