Proof in every caption is limited to the PT-## deliverables: what the $500 buys, the store in the client's name, the staged tests, the report, the post-launch corrections, the limits and the conditioned refund. The domain trust-tool scores (PT-16: ScamAdviser 0/100, ScamDoc 25%) are not answered in the ads. The page must answer them (C4 memo).
- **Refund procedure not on file:** PT-15 / F-10 give the window, its start, the coverage and the void triggers.
