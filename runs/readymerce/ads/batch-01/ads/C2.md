Store one sold posture correctors. I found the product the way a lot of people do, from a video of a guy saying it was about to blow up. I built the store over two weekends, turned on a $50 ad set, and waited.

When you do all that work just to find out people don't even want the thing you're selling, it's extremely discouraging. It makes you want to give up. So I did, faster than the first time. I had to stop or I was going to lose all my money.

It felt like pure loss. Money, time, energy, and honestly hope.

For months after that, if anyone brought up online stores, I'd say "I tried dropshipping" the way people say "I tried running." The videos make it seem like easy money when it's really not, and I had decided I wasn't one of the people it works for. I definitely was not an expert.

Five to ten. I had tested two, in two separate stores, and called myself a failure both times.

I didn't have two failed stores. I had two stores that each got one shot, and one guess misses a lot more often than it hits.

That felt lighter and worse. Lighter because it wasn't me. Worse because I still couldn't do anything with it. I couldn't afford five to ten more rounds of what I'd been doing, one product at a time with ads running until the money ran out. That way had cost me a thousand dollars for two answers.
