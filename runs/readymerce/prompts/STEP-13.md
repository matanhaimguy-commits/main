## 3 · DEFINITIONS (only the ones this step uses)

* **Positioning**: the mental slot the brand owns, built from four parts: the mechanism (M-2 in plain words) + the sub-avatar (a pocket the incumbents address lightly; a life situation, never a demographic) + a strategic enemy + an explicit "not for". Different, not better.
* **Strategic enemy**: a category, substance, industry, institution, habit or "the wrong diagnosis" the corpus already blames, never a named competitor.
* **Subcategory line**: "Not another [category], but for people who…" or "the first one that…". **Moat line**: what a copier cannot take: the avatar slot and the mechanism slot together. **Stranger-safe line**: the wedge stated so a reader who has never heard of any incumbent understands it.
* **STRICT-STORY ad**: an `IM-##` coded as a story-led native, not a product-forward static. **Independent network**: one advertiser operator (`NW-##`); alternate pages of one operator count once. **Authority read**: the 06 §8 verdict on experts: trusted, resented or mixed.
* **Narrator archetype**: who the story speaks through: fellow sufferer, family member/caregiver, insider who sees a pattern (hairdresser, coach, nurse), retired professional, founder, expert. **Disarm device**: what makes the narrator un-salesy: a kid narrator, a real review, a "let in on a secret" discovery frame. **Provenance tags** on every rendered person: REAL | SYNTHESIZED; on every credentialed voice: AUTHORITY — tags the team reads, never removals.
* **Native creative pattern** (`CR-##`): a visual concept named at this level: raw physical evidence of the problem, progress photo hiding a second story, ordinary object made meaningful by the copy, expert POV from where the problem is noticed, juxtaposition representing the mechanism.
* **Divergence axes**: palette, type, photography, casting, packaging, emotional temperature.
* **Catfish test**: the page repeats the ad's exact scenarios (ad says patella tendon, page cannot say arthritis).

## 6 · VALIDATION — what earns each label

1. **Positioning copy test.** The `stranger_safe_line` states the wedge without naming an incumbent; a line that needs the incumbent to make sense is a comparison → REWRITE once, then print FAIL.
2. **Both halves.** `mechanism_ref` AND `sub_avatar` filled → PASS; one only → HALF-POSITIONED. A statement missing the enemy or the not-for list prints INCOMPLETE beside the missing part.
3. **Enemy labels.** VALIDATED: ≥3 unique authors blame it across ≥2 threads (Q-##); SUPPORTED: 2 authors, or 1 author + ≥2 independent networks already attacking it (IM-##); HYPOTHESIS: seen only in ads or only analyst-derived; REJECTED (category error): a named competitor, an `LK-##` liked property, or an enemy contradicting a `live_wire: YES` belief.
4. **Not-for lines are real.** Each NF-## names an excluded cluster or a PT-## limitation; a line that excludes nobody the corpus contains is decoration → rewritten from the next `excluded_ref`, else labelled `DECORATION`.
5. **Narrator labels.** VALIDATED: the type carries STRICT-STORY ads in ≥2 independent above-floor networks AND the authority read does not contradict it; SUPPORTED: 1 network, or the authority read alone; HYPOTHESIS: neither; a type never seen in the market → EARLY SIGNAL; an expert narrator carries `AUTHORITY` and ranks by the authority read.
6. **Casting believability.** Every `casting_spec[AV]` traces to that avatar's Experience + Behaviour; a narrator whose daily reality is absent from the corpus → HYPOTHESIS with the missing scene named.
7. **Register congruence.** REG-1 matches ≥2 `CR-##` by name, uses only settings and objects with Q-## or Behaviour-scene provenance, and diverges on ≥2 DIV-1 axes → PASS. Copies the incumbents on every axis → FLAGGED "drowned out". Abandons every native trait → FLAGGED "reads as an ad".
8. **Casting provenance.** AI-generated faces may illustrate a scene, a narrator, a testimonial or a before/after; every rendered person carries REAL | SYNTHESIZED in the casting spec (keep a visible 3★ review — the sceptic uses it to decide the rest are real).
9. **Name status.** LOCKED when every clearance record is fetched and `product_anchor` PASS; anything less → PROVISIONAL with the missing record named. "No site found (SAMPLE)" is not a clearance record. A same-class registry hit is a printed red flag; the option still ships.
10. **Voice test + grade.** PASS when the three lines read as one speaker, all five samples sit at grade ≤7, contain no banned-word, and the ad-hook sample could not be said by a generic brand; FAIL prints the failing line.
11. **Story stamp.** POSITIONED carries no profession, illness or family-event claim about a named person and no tragedy, and names its discovery frame; REAL cites `[R-OWNED]`. Biography enters a POSITIONED story only as `[R-OWNED]`.
12. **Palette rule.** The CTA is the highest-contrast element on both backgrounds; body ≥16px on mobile; no colour-psychology assertion beyond contrast.
13. **Fact-sheet lint.** Every FACT-1 pointer resolves to an ID or prints `LATE-BOUND`; `components[]` never blank; `version` and `version_rule` present; `tiered n / untiered n / [A] n` printed.
