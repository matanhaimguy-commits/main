## 3 · DEFINITIONS (only the ones this step uses)

* **Avatar.** One person + her emotional pain + the failed attempts she paid for + the goal she wants + her daily habits + the scenes that repeat in the corpus. "30+ women with back pain" is a demographic. Avatars are discovered in the corpus, never invented at the desk.
* **Cluster (`CL-##`).** Unrelated people describing the same life circumstance driving them to the same problem — grouped by **situation + trigger**, never by age, wording, tone or subreddit. A `CP-##` enters as a SEEDED cluster.
* **Owned record.** A Q-## that belongs to this cluster and not equally to the nearest other. Every floor counts owned records only.
* **The four recurrence counters.** Observations · source units · unique authors · independent threads. One prolific poster is one author; one thread with forty replies is one thread.
* **The pond.** FISH = how many live this situation. BOATS = who already advertises to her. HUNGER = how badly they want out (triggers, seekers, unanswered-need posts, spend). BUYING POWER = what they already pay (wallet quotes, prices of the failed alternatives). "An untapped avatar is a big pond, few boats, hungry fish."
* **OWNED / STEALABLE / UNTAPPED / UNPROVEN.** Pond classes, each printed with the evidence line that earned it (section 6, rule 7). OWNED = independent networks already run dedicated ads to this person. STEALABLE = a network fishes it badly and the incumbent's own buyers say so. UNTAPPED = she exists, she is hungry, no network speaks to her. UNPROVEN = no fish read and no hunger read yet after the mining pass ran — written in full, ranked last, carrying the `HARVEST-##` that would prove it.
* **Competitor-avatar map (`CAM`).** Rows = every `NW-##`, columns = every `AV-##`, cell = PRIMARY (dedicated ads or funnel page) | SECONDARY (mentioned inside ads aimed elsewhere) | INCIDENTAL (casting only) | NONE, with the `IM-##` ids and longest `days_active`. Built from call-outs, transcripts, landing slugs and page names — never a language search.
* **Stacked characteristics.** The ≥3–4 traits that together make the avatar hers alone — beliefs, named apps or behaviours, product experiences, her own words for the problem.
* **Evidence tiers.** `[R-PAGE]` · `[R-SCRAPE]` · `[R-TOOL]` · `[R-WALLED]` (counted, not harvested) · `[R-SNIPPET]` (counts toward no floor) · `[R-PUB]` · `[R-OWNED]`.
* **Awareness rungs.** AW-1 Unaware · AW-2 Problem · AW-3 Solution · AW-4 Product · AW-5 Most Aware; cold money sits at 2–3. **Local stage** = the sophistication stage (1–5, per 07) inside ONE pond, which can sit lower than the category.
* **LF8.** 1 Survival · 2 Food and drink · 3 Freedom from fear, pain and danger · 4 Sexual companionship · 5 Comfortable living · 6 Being superior · 7 Protecting loved ones · 8 Social approval. ONE button, pressed hard; reliability order for physical products #3, #5, #7, #6, #4.
* **The eight route architectures** (for `route_hint`): pure causal chain · contradiction · "yes, but incomplete" · failed-solution · two-problem · pattern-discovery · comparison · delayed-effect.
* **Labels.** VALIDATED (independent, repeated evidence at threshold) · SUPPORTED (real evidence short of independence) · EARLY SIGNAL (1–2 speakers or one read) · HYPOTHESIS (ideated) · REJECTED (category error only — a demographic is not an avatar; a sibling differing only on age, wording, tone or subreddit is merged).
* **Honesty tokens.** `UNSIZED — method: [call]` · `NOT MAPPED` (tool total ≫ read) · `NULL — not in corpus` · `THIN — n quotes` · `INFERRED (from Q-##) H-##` · `WALLET: NULL — searched:` · `[D]` derived · `[A]` assumed (scored 5 by rule) · `SAMPLE(N)` · `LATE-BOUND: <file.field>` · `OPERATOR-PASTE`.

## 6 · VALIDATION — what earns each label

1. **Existence.** The same situation + trigger from ≥3 independent authors in ≥2 threads or sources, OR one heavily described case other posts echo. ≥3 authors + ≥2 threads → **VALIDATED**; ≥3 authors in one thread → **SUPPORTED**; 1–2 authors → **EARLY SIGNAL**; ideated with 0 records → **HYPOTHESIS** + `HARVEST-##`; differs from a sibling only on age, wording, tone or subreddit → merged (the only REJECTED). Evidence = the four recurrence counters on owned records.
2. **Core floor.** Desire ≥2 Q-## · Problem ≥2 · Belief ≥2 + "I believe…" statements marked ENTRENCHED | SOFT · Awareness ≥1 · Experience ≥1 + ≥3 named FS-## · Behaviour ≥2. All six met → **VALIDATED**; ≥4 of 6 → **SUPPORTED** (ship with `retrieved/target` per dimension); <4 → **EARLY SIGNAL** + `HARVEST-##` — the core is still written in full, `THIN — n quotes` on each short dimension. SPEAK-TO-ME prints PASS / FAIL: shown only the defining quote, trigger scene, failed history, outcome and objection, could she think it was written about someone else? Two clusters passing on the same five lines are one cluster.
3. **Fish.** Ads Manager bands: **<500K** small pond (fine for a test, hard to scale) · **1M–10M** sweet spot · **50M+** ocean, split further. Subreddits **10k–500k** members with posts in the last 7 days and >10 comments per thread; top-5 groups summed; pain-phrase TikTok **~5M views = hungry pond**; prevalence `[R-PUB]` with scope. ≥2 reads agreeing → **VALIDATED**; one → **SUPPORTED**; corpus counts only → **EARLY SIGNAL**; none → `UNSIZED`. Size is never read from upvotes or mention counts; the audience estimate is eligibility, not active users.
4. **Boats.** Two reads. (a) `advertisers_on_keyword` on `[product keyword] + [avatar word]`, ACTIVE: **0–5 = few boats, 20+ = crowded, needs a Stage 3–4 mechanism**; second ladder **2–5 @ 14–45 days = room, 50+ @ 90+ days = late**. (b) `networks_primary` from the CAM, alternate pages counting once. Longevity: **90+ days = a boat catching fish — study it**; 45+ almost certainly profitable; ≤7 days says nothing. Both reads + a coverage line → **VALIDATED**; one → **SUPPORTED**; neither → `NOT MAPPED`, never "clear". "Untapped" is never read from a forum-sentence query returning nothing; longevity proves spend, not a copyable creative.
5. **Hunger.** Count `trigger` Q-##, seeker / recent-buyer share, unanswered-need posts, spend quotes; pain score pre- and post-harvest. Emotional wording alone is insufficient. ≥3 triggers + ≥1 seeker + ≥1 unanswered post → **VALIDATED**; two of three → **SUPPORTED**; one → **EARLY SIGNAL**; zero after the pass → **HYPOTHESIS** + `HARVEST-##` (the pond reads UNPROVEN). Never read from member counts or one prolific poster.
6. **Buying power.** ≥5 owned wallet quotes with amounts → **VALIDATED**; 3–4 → **SUPPORTED**; 1–2 → **EARLY SIGNAL**; 0 → `WALLET: NULL — searched: [queries]`.
7. **Class (a label with an evidence line, never a removal):**
