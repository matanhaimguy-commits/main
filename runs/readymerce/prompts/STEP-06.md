## 3 · DEFINITIONS (only the ones this step uses)

* **Q-record** — one row of `06-VOC_MASTER.csv` = one coded verbatim observation from one speaker. ID `Q-<S>-####`, `S` ∈ `R` Reddit · `E` review platforms (Amazon, Trustpilot, retailer) · `Y` YouTube · `T` TikTok · `G` Facebook groups, pages, ad comments · `Q` Quora · `F` niche forums · `S` other public social · `B` blog comments · `O` other first-person (survey, DM, operator paste). `C-####` = a contextual or expert record, never VOC, never in a language bank.
* **Source unit** — one thread, review page, video comment set, post comment set or question page; the counter increments per unit.
* **Three corpora, never mixed** — `MARKET_VOC` (people living the problem) · `PRODUCT_TRUTH` (buyers of the identical SKU) · `OWNED_PROOF` (the operator's own survey, DMs, ad comments).
* **Evidence tiers** — `[R-PAGE]` fetched page · `[R-SCRAPE]` scraped dataset · `[R-SNIPPET]` search snippet (counts toward no floor) · `[R-WALLED]` login-walled · `[R-TOOL]` tool figure · `[R-PUB]` published source · `[D]` derived · `[A]` assumed.
* **The four recurrence ledgers** — per theme, PB, CP and every FS/OBJ/MC/AD candidate: `observations · unique_source_units · unique_authors · independent_threads`, argued in the order **unique authors > independent threads > source units > raw observations**, plus `platforms_n`. One prolific poster is one author however many times they post.
* **Evidence class** — `VERBATIM_VOC` (the person states an experience, wish, belief or action) · `REPORTED_OUTCOME` (something happened) · `CUSTOMER_EXPLANATION` (their theory of why). A report establishes that they said it, not that their explanation is correct.
* **Solution status** — `TRIED-FAILED | TRIED-HELPED | TRIED-HELPED-THEN-RETURNED | TRIED-UNCLEAR | CURRENTLY-USING | CONSIDERING | REFUSES | STOPPED-SIDE-EFFECTS | RECOMMENDED-BY-OTHERS-ONLY | NULL`; filled whenever a solution is mentioned. "Mentioned" is never "tried".
* **Awareness stage** — `AW-1` unaware · `AW-2` problem-aware · `AW-3` solution-aware · `AW-4` product-aware · `AW-5` most-aware · `NULL` when the passage is silent; coded as the highest level the passage itself demonstrates, with `awareness_ref` naming the product or category referred to (§6.6).
* **Speaker stage** — `sufferer | passive | seeker | recent_buyer | satisfied | dissatisfied | veteran | former | caregiver | unknown` + `speaker_locator` (≤10 words); a journey stage, never mapped to awareness.
* **hyper_responsive** — the top 20% longest, most specific records per PB; length is the leading indicator of a buyer.
* **Saturation labels** — `STRONG` (the last 3 source units added no new code AND the theme is VALIDATED) · `MODERATE` (new codes still appear, VALIDATED) · `EARLY SIGNAL` (SUPPORTED or below) · `INSUFFICIENT` (<3 records). Labels sit beside counts, never replace them.
* **Reading order inside a page (15 tiers)** — original post → highly upvoted comments → detailed firsthand experiences → emotionally intense comments → specific physical/practical experiences → failed solutions → solution objections → frustration with repeated advice → long-term follow-ups → recovery/success → considering quitting → already quit → experiences contradicting standard explanations → identity/confidence/social consequences → explicit desires and fears. Skip "me too" and generic advice without firsthand experience.
* `PARTIAL (N=n/floor) — chain exhausted: […]` — printed when a floor is unmet after its URL list, fallback chain and `max_extra_sources` new URLs, or when the box ends; the work proceeds.

## 6 · VALIDATION — what earns each label

1. **Record integrity.** `exact_quote` copy-pasted; omissions "…"; speakers never joined; paraphrase only in `notes` labelled PARAPHRASE; `source_url` + `unique_author_id` + `date_posted` present → **VALIDATED record**; missing any → `[R-SNIPPET]`, counts toward no floor.
2. **Theme recurrence** is claimed only from the ledgers: **VALIDATED** at ≥3 unique authors across ≥3 independent threads on ≥2 platforms · **SUPPORTED** at ≥3 authors on one platform · **EARLY SIGNAL** at 1–2 authors · **HYPOTHESIS** (printed `ANECDOTE`) when every observation traces to one author, however upvoted.
3. **Depth flag.** `hyper_responsive` records feed the verbatim banks first; frequency ranks themes, depth chooses quotes.
4. **Fact-grade vs hypothesis-grade.** "What I tried / don't want / paid / what happened" records (`failed_solution`, `refuses`, `spend`, `outcome_reported`) are fact-grade; "what I want" (`desire`) is a hypothesis for 08; an emotion is never relabelled as a desire.
5. **Solution status.** `TRIED-*` only when the person reports trying it AND an outcome; secondhand = `RECOMMENDED-BY-OTHERS-ONLY`; untried = `CONSIDERING`.
6. **Awareness per record.** The highest level the passage demonstrates; NULL when silent; competitor knowledge ≠ awareness of our brand; a review is purchaser evidence, not AW-5; 07 decides dominance.
7. **Floors route work; they never end the run.** An unmet floor triggers the lane's fallback chain and up to `max_extra_sources` new URLs (added to 05 first) inside the box, then `PARTIAL (N=n/floor) — chain exhausted: […]`; the report proceeds.
8. **Assumption verdicts.** Contradicting quotes searched as hard as confirming ones; one targeted query per unconfirmed `AS-##` before UNSEEN.
