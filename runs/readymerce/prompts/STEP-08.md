## 3 · DEFINITIONS (only the ones this step uses)

**Channel, never create.** A mass desire is "the public spread of a private want that already exists"; "A Mass Desire is the life destination the customer wants to move toward, or the feared future they desperately want to avoid." "Untapped desires don't exist"; only untapped avatars and mechanisms do — a want no brand sells is a diagnosis to route, never a prize.

**The desire hierarchy (a finding is a Mass Desire only at the last rung):** Problem (cannot create winning ads) → Immediate Desired Result (better ads) → Functional Outcome (profitable advertising) → Broader Outcome (a business making far more money) → Mass Desire ("I want to quit my 9-to-5 job"); beneath it sit the Emotional payoff and the Root human motivation. Rung codes: `problem | immediate_result | functional_outcome | broader_outcome | mass_desire | emotional_payoff | root`.

**Three rungs, three objects, none exportable as another:** `lf8_root` (why it never satiates) → `MD-##` (the bedrock engine the strategist tests) → `DS-##` (the recognizable line the copywriter writes). The DS text ends at the phrase the customer "genuinely recognizes, wants, and would plausibly say out loud"; only the MD descends to bedrock ("why do they want that?" until the question sounds stupid) and is stated as the vague, product-agnostic, market-level feeling with BOTH poles, `feel ___ / no longer feel ___`: the vagueness is what makes it mass; specificity lives in the scenes, never in the desire.

**The "I want to" test:** a usable desire is expressible as "I want to…". Weak → stronger: Confidence → "I want to wear fitted clothes again without hiding my body"; Better health → "I want to keep traveling with my spouse instead of planning our life around what my body can no longer do."

**The abstraction lint.** None of *freedom, confidence, happiness, control, empowerment, security, peace of mind, success, recognition, feeling normal, better health, more energy, pain relief, weight loss, thicker hair, better skin, profitability, more money, independence* may stand as a terminal desire. Seven probes: what would having this let them DO; QUIT doing; REGAIN; PROTECT; PROVE; who would they BECOME; what feared future would they AVOID. Bind the answer to a Q-## or H-##; otherwise print `NO-DEEPER-EVIDENCE — searched:` and keep the abstraction stamped. "Do not force a deeper interpretation when customer evidence does not support one."

**The desire TIER RULE (three tests — a label, never a verdict; a candidate that fails any test is still written in full as an MD-## stamped HYPOTHESIS, with the desire underneath it):** T1 STRIP THE SITUATION — restate it with no product, no problem, no backstory and none of *this / again / back / still / permanent / anymore*; does it still describe something humans want? ("feel attractive" passes; "be believed" and "feel like myself again" collapse.) T2 PRE-EXISTING — is it true of millions who never had this problem and never heard of the product? "You'll know it's the correct mass desire if an unaware person can have it"; "the problem that your product solves is not the mass desire". T3 CAN'T BE SATISFIED — could one fact, one acknowledgment or one purchase permanently end it? If yes it is an objection resolving, not a bottomless drive. FAIL labels: `DEMAND` (an outcome the product delivers), `SITUATIONAL` (collapses without the backstory), `QUALIFIER` (a solution-selection criterion), `BELIEF` (a proposition to be convinced of); each FAIL names the real pre-existing desire underneath, or "none".

**The eight conditions (the Mass Desire Quality Standard), each count-bound:** REPEATED (≥3 distinct speakers); CONCRETE (≥1 DS-## at rung ≥ mass_desire); EMOTIONALLY LOADED (≥1 `emotion:` Q-##); PROBLEM-LINKED (≥1 `blocks_desire`); PERSISTENT (≥2 records ≥30 days apart, or UNKNOWN); SOLUTION-SEEKING RELEVANT (≥1 seeker or recent_buyer speaker); CROSS-CONTEXTUAL (≥2 source types or communities); OBSERVABLE (≥1 `scene`). A condition without its count is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, an allowed rating. "Do not classify something as a dominant desire merely because it sounds psychologically plausible."

**The nine ranking criteria are the counts**, printed Very High / High / Moderate / Low / Insufficient Evidence beside each; no summed score ("Do not fake mathematical precision"): Prevalence = REPEATED; Emotional Intensity = EMOTIONALLY LOADED; Desire Magnitude = magnitude; Desire Heat = heat; Concreteness = CONCRETE; Problem Proximity = PROBLEM-LINKED; Solution-Seeking Proximity = SOLUTION-SEEKING; Evidence Diversity = CROSS-CONTEXTUAL; Persistence = PERSISTENT. "Explain why each desire outranks the next."

**Magnitude vs heat, always assessed separately:** magnitude = how important the outcome is to the customer's broader life, computed from the four recurrence counters on owned records (unique authors > independent threads > source units > observations); heat = how emotionally active it is during solution-seeking, computed from owned `trigger`-tagged Q-## plus owned seeker / recent_buyer records. Bands: magnitude by unique authors ≥10 Very High, 6–9 High, 3–5 Moderate, 1–2 Low, 0 Insufficient; heat by records ≥8 Very High, 5–7 High, 2–4 Moderate, 1 Low, 0 Insufficient.

**Life Force 8 (roots, never the stated desire):** 1 survival, enjoyment of life, life extension; 2 enjoyment of food and beverages; 3 freedom from fear, pain and danger; 4 sexual companionship; 5 comfortable living conditions; 6 superiority, winning, achievement, keeping up socially; 7 care and protection of loved ones; 8 social approval and acceptance. An ad presses ONE button hard; the research object carries every anchored route. Reliability order for physical products, a tie-breaker prior only: #3, #5, #7, #6, #4. "Primal beats learned." The NINE LEARNED SECONDARY WANTS (being informed, curiosity, cleanliness, efficiency, convenience, dependability or quality, expression of beauty or style, economy or profit, bargains) "may support solution choice. They should not replace the concrete Mass Desire" — routed to 12, never roots. The six TECHNOLOGICAL PROBLEMS (complexity, overwhelm, fragility, maintenance, incompatibility, obsolescence) are a product-aware lens routed to 10 and 12. FORCE OF CHANGE (mass education | style trend | NONE) is an optional tag.

**Mindstates and orientation.** "A mindstate is temporary. It is not a permanent demographic identity or personality type." Nine motivations: Achievement, Autonomy, Belonging, Competence, Empowerment, Engagement, Esteem, Nurturance, Security; research the situations that activate them. Promotion orientation = gain, reach a better state, move toward an aspiration; prevention = avoid failure, prevent loss, protect what they have, move away from a feared future; one desire may appear in both.

**Dossier terms:** NEGATIVE MIRROR = the feared opposite, completed as "I am afraid that…"; IDENTITY PERMISSION = how they justify wanting it without violating self-image; DESIRE CONFLICT / TRADEOFF = "I want [result], but I do not want to [sacrifice, risk, endure, or become]" (the AD-## anti-desires); PUBLIC vs PRIVATE REASON = what they say publicly vs admit anonymously ("Do not invent private motives"); ACTIVATION EVENT types = a photograph, a comment, a doctor visit, missing an event, being unable to do a familiar task, seeing peers succeed, a worsening symptom, an upcoming wedding / reunion / trip / birthday / holiday, a relationship interaction, another failed solution; SIDE-OF-THE-STREET TEST = would a real person answer "why do you care about fixing this?" with this line?

**Crystallization, the two-halves rule.** THE LACK is verbatim-rich because research is done on people still in the pain: every lack scene is a Q-## (sharpening allowed, still cited). THE FULFILLMENT will rarely have verbatim: script each fulfillment scene as the mirror-image of a documented lack scene (she takes one mug down → she reaches for two), built ONLY from her real documented details, labelled `H-## [scripted from her life: Q-##, Q-##]`; never write "no corpus support" on the fulfillment side. Technique MENU (Schwartz's 13): direct sensory; in action; her first day; how she proves it to herself; days → weeks → months; an audience witnessing; experts registering it (fulfillment half only as a real Q-##; an H-## never places a credentialed witness); compared against FS-##; the black side; every daily touchpoint; a metaphor; the catalog of satisfactions; the guarantee at the trust wound.

## 6 · VALIDATION — what earns each label

1. **A DS-## is verbatim or [D]-normalised from a cited Q-## / IM-##**; an inferred want is `ANALYST INFERENCE` bound to its Q-##, never presented as said; reported outcomes are never desires. "A complaint is evidence of a problem; the underlying desire is an interpretation unless stated."
2. **T1–T3 all pass → eligible for VALIDATED / SUPPORTED / EARLY SIGNAL; any fail → HYPOTHESIS**, the dossier written in full with the FAIL label and the desire underneath, ranked last. This is the tier rule.
3. **Tier by conditions:** T1–T3 pass AND ≥6 of 8 including REPEATED, CONCRETE, PROBLEM-LINKED and SOLUTION-SEEKING → VALIDATED; pass AND 4–5 of 8 → SUPPORTED; pass AND ≤3 of 8, or one community only → EARLY SIGNAL (listed, unranked); any T fail, or ADVERTISER-ONLY with 0 owned Q-##, or an LF8 route with no verbatim → HYPOTHESIS; REJECTED (`RJ-##`) only for an outcome or benefit mislabelled as a desire.
4. **Ranking criteria are the counts, not a second score**; no summed score, no weights.
5. **Advertiser validation is read from 03's label, never recomputed here**; VALIDATED needs ≥2 independent networks; reuse inside one network is repetition evidence, not market validation; runtime is evidence, not causation; the two stamps never seed each other.
6. **Class rules** apply after tiering; a CORPUS-ONLY desire answers the vacancy question ("is ANY brand, anywhere, in any adjacent category, monetising this want?") before UNCLAIMED prints; no and a small population → `UNCLAIMED — possibly no money in it`; every CORPUS-ONLY row is routed to 09 (an untapped population?) and 10 (an unnamed mechanism?) before 12 spends on it.
7. **Product leverage is a separate later judgment** (a huge desire the product barely touches does not lead): High = a PT-## line directly produces the DS-## outcome; Medium = produces a precondition; Low = plausible only via a mechanism 10 has not yet built.
8. **Side-of-the-street test** on every `top_ds`; a NO rewrites the DS (the MD stays). Descent ends at the recognizable phrase for the DS rung and at bedrock for the MD rung.
9. **Every fulfillment IB-## carries its Q-## provenance list**; a scene with a detail in no cited Q-## is cut, not smoothed. "Do not invent thoughts."
