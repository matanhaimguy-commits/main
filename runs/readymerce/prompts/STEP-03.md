## 3 · DEFINITIONS

* **Taxonomy (do not blur these together)** — **Problem**: the pain, symptom, conflict, frustration, fear or lived situation dramatised. **Outcome**: the direct end state created when the product solves the problem; not automatically the Desire. **Desire**: the conscious thing the customer wants that the Outcome helps them get — one level beyond the Outcome, no deeper than what they would say out loud. **Existing knowledge / belief**: what competitor ads assume the prospect already knows, believes, has heard or has tried. **Big Idea**: the entire sales argument in one intuitive, shareable sentence — never a bare mechanism label. **Angle**: a distinct persuasive lens used to enter the Big Idea; sub-types `MASTER PROMISE | MECHANISM | EMOTIONAL ENTRY | OTHER`. **Concept**: the actual story or scenario. **Mechanism**: why the problem persists or why the solution should work. **Story structure**: the repeatable sequence of narrative beats. **Hook**: the opening device that creates the open loop. **Creative pattern**: the visual concept, never the format. **Persona**: who the story speaks through or to.
* **The collapse test** — *"If I changed the narrator, hook, creative, setting and story details, would the ad still make the same persuasive argument?"* Yes → the same angle or Big Idea.
* **Materially different concepts** — the story or scenario differs, not the narrator or hook; DCO variants never count.
* **The Side-of-the-Street Test** — *"Why do you care about fixing this problem?" — would a real person in this market plausibly answer with this Desire? If not, rewrite it.*
* **Control / challengers / territory / hole** — the control is the argument the most money is riding on; challengers are the next arguments beside it; a territory is a validated space; a hole is a persona, lane or desire NO network sells after the searches that established it.
* **Two stamps, kept apart** — `market_validation` (advertisers sell it; this step) and `corpus_evidence` (buyers hold it; Q-## in steps 06/08); neither seeds the other. Every row minted here carries `corpus_evidence: PENDING-06`.
* **Longevity labels** (inherited from 02) — `BET <14 · LIKELY 14–44 · PROVEN 45–89 · CONTROL-GRADE ≥90 · OWNED ≥180`.
* **Product fit** — `product_fit: YES | INFERRED | UNKNOWN`: whether PT-## / F-## (or, while ours are inferred, `components[]` + `competitor_components[]`) let OUR product make the argument. A column on every VD / VA / BI / MT / ST row, never an exclusion.

## 5 · METHOD (do these in order)

1. **Merge** (`MODE: MERGE`; object 0). Print `partials n of expected · NW n → n after dedupe · IM n · NEW SEED n (NOT CRAWLED n)`.
2. **Ingest + lint.** Load `02-swipe.csv` and the step-01 objects (DEEP fields `LATE-BOUND` if absent); print `IM n · STRICT n · networks n (ABOVE-FLOOR n) · lanes n · personas n`. Run the taxonomy lint over the IM fields (a `big_idea` that is a hook; a `desire_sold` that is an outcome) and fix, citing the ad id.
3. **Collapse.** Group IM records by `CO-##`; collapse `CO-##` into `BI-##` with the collapse test; print the compression line; re-open ambiguous ads with `get_ad`. **Counter:** `CO n → BI n`.
4. **Cluster desires.** Per ad ask: *"If the product solved the problem exactly as promised, what conscious thing would the customer believe that allows them to get, do, feel, avoid, preserve or experience?"* Cluster materially different concepts under one desire; never merge different desires that share an outcome. Apply section 6 rule 5; write the bridge, negative mirror and representative lines.
5. **Score + label.** Compute the 40 / 25 / 20 / 15 score and the rule-2 label in code for VD / VA / BI; assign product leverage against PT-## / F-##; print the DESIRE LEADERBOARD and the `primary_desire_candidate` line; list `desires_expected_but_not_validated[]` against SD-##. **Counter:** `VD n (VALIDATED n / SUPPORTED n / EARLY SIGNAL n / HYPOTHESIS n)`.
6. **Angles.** Name each distinct persuasive lens; type it; run the collapse test; attach evidence; write the product-fit line; fill `what_is_not_an_angle[]`.
7. **Big Ideas.** Per BI-##: every object-5 field.
8. **Mechanism territories.** From `incumbent_mechanism_map[]` + `stated_cause_counts`: territories with saturation reads; `product_can_own` from `mechanism_capacity` and F-## (`INFERRED` while ours are inferred); never invented, `UNKNOWN` allowed.
9. **Structures.** From STRICT-STORY records only: extract skeletons, cluster, rank per desire by nine criteria in order — runtime, active persistence, performance, reuse / duplication, number of materially different executions, independent advertiser support, strength of desire fit, adaptability to our product, quality of the native story structure — into `Strongest · Second strongest · Useful secondary · Emerging / speculative`; write the constant / variable lines; build the Desire × Structure matrix. **Counter:** `ST n · matrix rows n`.
10. **Hooks, creative, proof.** Abstract `hook_bank_raw[]` into HK-## (template + feeling, never the words); CR-## from `native_creative_description` and keyframes; PR-## from `proof_forms[]`; the strongest stack from the longest-running strict ads; narrator archetype counts.
11. **Offers + funnels.** OF-## from `offer_funnel_table[]`; funnel by entry stage; the penetration offer.
12. **Beliefs + starting point.** EK-## from `assumed_knowledge[]` and attacked beliefs; entry-stage share as counts; `product_appearance_median`; the sophistication-tell counts over the 20 longest-running ads; the "Do not begin by teaching X…" instruction.
13. **Avatar × desire × angle + holes.** Cross `avatar_coverage_map` with VD / VA; mark VACANT personas, unsold lanes and unsold desires as `holes[]`; answer the vacancy question per hole from `keyword_advertiser_bands[]` and adjacent-category evidence in the corpus; label each hole HYPOTHESIS. **Counter:** `holes n (personas n / lanes n / desires n)`.
14. **Product-fit pass.** Every VD / VA / BI / MT / ST row gets `product_fit` (§3); every row stays eligible; control and challengers are chosen on market evidence, `product_fit` printed beside each.
15. **Territory map + control / challengers.** Rank by networks → concept breadth → longevity → reuse; choose the angle control + 3 challengers and the BI × ST control + 3–5 challenger combinations, each pairing justified; list emerging and signal-only territories; name `best_strategic_territory`. **Counter:** `control 1 + challengers n · combos 1 + n · emerging n · signal-only n`.
16. **Bottom lines.** Fill both formulas with ids; write both five-sentence Bottom Lines.
17. **One-sheet + QC.** Write the twelve sections + BEFORE YOU WRITE in ≤1 page; run the taxonomy QC (rule 10) and the 5-minute test; consolidate overlaps.
18. **Swipe file + close.** Select 10–20 strict + ≤10 all-format rows; write the swipe fields; `save_ad_to_swipe_file`; write `03-VALIDATED-MESSAGING.md` with the coverage statement and `03-HANDOFF.json`.

## 6 · VALIDATION — what earns each label

1. **The scoring weights** (computed in code per VD-##, applied identically to VA-## and BI-##): Independent Advertiser Breadth **40%**, Concept Breadth **25%** (materially different concepts), Longevity / Persistence **20%**, Performance / Reuse **15%** (Winning / Optimized / Scaling, `used_count`, duplication). Inputs per row: `networks_n, concepts_n, max_days, proven_ads_n, reuse_n, winning_n`, normalised to the corpus maximum. The score only orders rows; the label is the evidence statement; ties break by networks, then longest ad.
2. **The labels** (computed in code): `VALIDATED` = ≥3 independent ABOVE-FLOOR networks AND ≥2 materially different concepts AND ≥1 ad ≥60 days; also VALIDATED, printed `VALIDATED-2NW` = ≥2 networks AND ≥1 ad ≥45 days · `SUPPORTED` = ≥2 networks with any serious ads, OR one network with ≥5 records on the argument, `reuse = Y` and ≥1 ad ≥60 days (printed `SINGLE-NETWORK ITERATED`, never VALIDATED) · `EARLY SIGNAL` = ≥1 network, ≥2 executions, every ad <45 days · `HYPOTHESIS` = one execution, a step-01 candidate with no ad, or zero executions after the searches · `REJECTED` = the one category error: it fails the collapse test (a hook, persona or setting is not an angle or Big Idea). Product fit never changes a label.
3. **Independence.** Counts are of NW-## ABOVE-FLOOR, never pages; reuse inside one network is repetition evidence, not independent market validation.
4. **The collapse test** decides angles and Big Ideas; materially different concepts per section 3.
5. **Desire acceptance (all six, or REWRITE):** a real customer might say it, consciously understood, matters enough to influence purchase, exactly one meaningful level beyond the product outcome, broad enough for a segment, distinct from a scene, hook, mechanism, objection or attribute; then the Side-of-the-Street Test. **The six shapes that get REWRITE:** too complicated, outcome-centric ("I want my hair back" — ask why), context-specific (a scene, kept for copy), "something that works" (a selection criterion), product-centric (an objection or belief), too deep. Never merge different desires that share an outcome. Final wording is customer or competitor language, or a simple normalisation of it.
6. **Product leverage.** How naturally and credibly OUR product delivers the Outcome that enables this Desire, against PT-## / F-##. A VALIDATED desire with LOW leverage stays VALIDATED and ranks down for test priority. While the truth card is `LATE-BOUND`, leverage prints `INFERRED` from `components[]`.
7. **Structures** come only from STRICT-STORY records coded from full bodies or transcripts; a structure needs ≥2 materially different ads (≥2 networks for VALIDATED); protagonists and settings are not structures.
8. **Hook, creative and proof patterns** are listed when they recur across ≥2 networks or sit on ≥1 PROVEN (≥45-day) ad; a "market pattern" claim needs ≥60% of ≥45-day ads; the abstracted template preserves the feeling, not the words.
9. **Existing beliefs.** Evidence is ad text only; every EK-## prints `ads assume X — corpus does not confirm (PENDING-06); do not begin there` until step 06 stamps it; the three kinds are kept apart; this step writes `market_validation` only.
10. **Taxonomy lint** (printed PASS / FAIL per object): Problem ≠ Desire, existing belief ≠ new information, Big Idea ≠ Mechanism ≠ Hook ≠ Story Structure ≠ Creative Pattern ≠ Persona, structure ≠ setting or protagonist, repeated execution inside one network ≠ independent validation, competitor claim ≠ fact, a validated market desire ≠ a validated product claim.
